# Showing tax IDs on invoices

Ledgerly can print your tax ID on every invoice, and can also show your client's tax ID. Many countries require a tax number on invoices, so check what applies to you.

## Where do I enter my own tax ID?

1. Open **Settings → Invoices**.
2. Scroll to **Business details**.
3. Enter your registration number in **Tax ID**.
4. Edit **Tax ID label** if you want a name other than "Tax ID", such as "VAT number", "GST number" or "EIN".
5. Click **Save**.

The ID appears in the header of the invoice, under your business name and address, on all three templates (Classic, Modern and Compact). It also appears on the PDF and on the online payment page.

## How do I add a client's tax ID?

Every client record has a **Tax ID** field. Open **Clients**, click the client, choose **Edit**, and fill in the Tax ID field. When you create an invoice for that client, their tax ID is printed in the "Bill to" block.

This matters for business clients that need their own registration number on invoices to reclaim tax. It is also needed for reverse-charge invoices, described in Zero-rated and reverse-charge notes.

## Which invoices show the tax ID?

- New invoices show your current Tax ID and the client's current Tax ID.
- Invoices already sent keep the details they were sent with. If you correct a typo later, the sent invoice is not rewritten.
- Draft invoices pick up changes the next time you open and save them.

If you need to correct a sent invoice, void it and create a new one. Voided invoice numbers are not reused, as explained in the invoice status articles.

## Can I hide the tax ID?

Yes. In **Settings → Invoices → Business details**, leave the Tax ID field empty and nothing is printed. For a single client, leave their Tax ID field empty. There is no setting to show the ID on only some invoices.

## What format should the ID be in?

Ledgerly accepts any text up to 50 characters, including letters, numbers, spaces and dashes. It does not validate the number against any government registry, so type it exactly as it was issued to you.

| Field | Where to edit | Max length |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your Tax ID | Settings → Invoices → Business details | 50 characters |
| Tax ID label | Settings → Invoices → Business details | 30 characters |
| Client Tax ID | Clients → client → Edit | 50 characters |

Ledgerly does not file taxes or give tax advice. Confirm which details your invoices must contain with your tax authority or accountant.
