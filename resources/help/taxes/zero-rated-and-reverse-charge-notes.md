# Zero-rated and reverse-charge notes

Ledgerly supports zero-rated sales and reverse-charge invoices by combining a 0% tax rate with footer text. There is no special reverse-charge switch, so you add the wording your tax rules require yourself.

## What do zero-rated and reverse charge mean?

A **zero-rated** sale is a taxable sale charged at 0%. The sale still counts for reporting, but no tax is collected. A **reverse charge** means the buyer accounts for the tax instead of the seller. It is commonly used for certain business-to-business sales across borders, and invoices usually carry a note saying so.

Which situation applies to you depends on your location and your client's. Ledgerly does not give tax advice, so confirm with your tax authority or accountant.

## How do I create a zero-rated invoice?

1. Open **Settings → Taxes** and click **Add tax rate**.
2. Name it "Zero-rated" and enter a rate of 0.
3. Choose **Exclusive** and click **Save**.
4. On the invoice, select "Zero-rated" in the **Tax** field of each applicable line.

Using a real 0% rate, rather than leaving the field empty, means these sales show up in the Tax summary report under the "Zero-rated" name, which makes them easy to total for your return.

## How do I add a reverse-charge note?

1. Add the client's tax ID on their record, under **Clients → client → Edit → Tax ID**.
2. Apply your 0% rate to the lines.
3. Open the invoice and edit the **Footer text** field.
4. Type the wording required where you operate, for example "Reverse charge: tax to be accounted for by the recipient."
5. Save and send.

To use the same note on every invoice, set it once in **Settings → Invoices → Footer text**. Footer text set on an individual invoice replaces the account default for that invoice only.

## Can I keep different notes for different clients?

The account-wide footer applies to all invoices. If only some clients need a reverse-charge note, leave the account footer for general text and add the note on each relevant invoice. Recurring invoices copy the footer from the schedule, so set it when you create the schedule.

## What shows on the invoice?

The 0% rate appears in the tax summary with an amount of 0.00. Your footer text appears at the bottom of the page and on the PDF. Both appear on the client's online payment page too.

| Need | Setup |
| --- | --- |
| Zero-rated sale | 0% rate on the line |
| Reverse charge | 0% rate, client tax ID, footer note |
| Fully exempt client | Tax-exempt option on client record |

For clients who are always exempt, see Tax-exempt clients.
