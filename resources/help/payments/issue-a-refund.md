# Issue a refund

You can refund an online payment in full or in part within 90 days of the payment date. The refund goes back to the method the client used, and the original processing fee is not returned.

## Which payments can I refund?

Refunds are available for payments taken through Ledgerly, meaning card and ACH payments made on the payment page. They are possible for 90 days after the payment date. After 90 days the Refund button is no longer shown, and you would need to return the money yourself and record the adjustment.

Payments you recorded manually, such as cash or a check, are not refundable inside Ledgerly because Ledgerly never held the money. Remove or correct those through the invoice instead.

## How do I issue a refund?

1. Open **Payments** in the sidebar and find the payment, or open the invoice and scroll to its payment history.
2. Click the payment, then **Refund**.
3. Choose **Full refund** or **Partial refund**. For a partial refund, enter the amount, up to the amount of the original payment.
4. Add an optional internal reason, such as "Service cancelled".
5. Click **Confirm refund**.

A refund cannot be undone once confirmed. You can issue several partial refunds on one payment as long as the total stays within the original amount.

## When does the client get the money?

Ledgerly sends the refund at once, but the time it takes to show in the client's account depends on their bank. Card refunds usually appear within 5 to 10 business days. ACH refunds usually take around 5 business days. The client also receives an emailed notice of the refund.

## What happens to the invoice?

The invoice balance is adjusted by the refunded amount.

| Situation | Result |
|---|---|
| Full refund of a fully paid invoice | Status returns to Sent, or Overdue if the due date has passed |
| Partial refund | Outstanding balance increases by the refund amount |
| Refund where you also want to cancel the invoice | Refund first, then use **⋯ → Void** |

If the invoice becomes Overdue again, reminders and late fees follow the usual rules. To avoid that, void the invoice or mark it as disputed (**⋯ → Mark as disputed**), which pauses reminders and late fees.

## Are fees returned?

No. The 2.9% + 30¢ card fee or the 0.8% ACH fee charged on the original payment stays with the payment. The refund amount is deducted from your next payout. If a payout would go below zero, the balance carries over and is taken from later payments.

## Should I refund or issue a credit?

Refund when the client was charged for something they will not receive. If instead they will keep working with you, you can leave the money in place and discount a future invoice. Ledgerly does not hold client credit balances, so apply the discount as a line or percent on the next invoice.
