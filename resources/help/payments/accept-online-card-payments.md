# Accept online card payments

You can let clients pay invoices by card directly from the secure payment page. Online payments are available on the Pro and Business plans and need a connected bank account for payouts.

## How do I turn on card payments?

1. Go to **Settings → Payments**.
2. Under **Payout account**, connect your bank account if you have not already. See Connect your bank account for payouts.
3. In the **Payment methods** section, switch **Credit and debit cards** on.
4. Click **Save**.

From then on, every invoice you email includes a secure payment link, and the payment page shows a card form. You can switch the method off again at any time without affecting past payments.

## What does a card payment cost?

Ledgerly charges 2.9% + 30¢ per successful charge. There is no monthly fee on top of your plan. For example:

| Invoice | Fee | You receive |
|---|---|---|
| $100.00 | $3.20 | $96.80 |
| $500.00 | $14.80 | $485.20 |
| $2,000.00 | $58.30 | $1,941.70 |

Failed or declined payments cost nothing. The fee is deducted before the money reaches your payout. See Processing fees for the full list.

## What does my client see?

When the client opens the payment link, they see the invoice summary, the amount due (including any late fee shown as its own line), and the card form. After paying:

- The invoice updates to Paid within a minute if the full balance was paid.
- A receipt is emailed to the client automatically.
- You see the payment under **Payments** in the sidebar.

Card numbers are never stored by Ledgerly.

## What if the payment is declined?

The client sees a message from their card issuer and can try another card. The invoice stays Sent or Overdue and reminders continue on their normal schedule. If a client says they were charged but the invoice still shows unpaid, check the **Payments** list. If nothing is there, the payment did not complete and they should try again.

## Can clients pay part of an invoice?

Yes. A partial payment reduces the outstanding balance, and the invoice stays Sent or Overdue until the balance reaches zero. The payment page then shows the remaining amount.

## Why can't I see the card option?

Check these, in order:

- You are on Pro or Business (or in the 14-day trial).
- A payout account is connected and verified.
- **Credit and debit cards** is switched on in Settings → Payments.
- Your country is supported. See Supported countries and currencies for payments.
- The invoice currency is one of the currencies Ledgerly supports.

If everything looks right and the option is still missing, contact support@ledgerly.example.
