# Payout schedule

Money from online payments is sent to your connected bank account within 2 business days. Your first payout can take up to 7 days.

## When do payouts arrive?

After a client pays online, the funds clear and are then paid out automatically. Normally the money lands in your bank account within 2 business days of the payment. Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding bank holidays in your payout country.

| Payment made | Typical arrival |
|---|---|
| Monday | Wednesday |
| Thursday | Monday |
| Friday | Tuesday |
| Saturday or Sunday | Wednesday |

Your own bank may take a little longer to show the deposit.

## Why is my first payout slower?

The first payout can take up to 7 days. During this period Ledgerly confirms your account details and the first successful payment. Every payout after the first follows the normal 2 business day schedule.

## What counts toward a payout?

Each payout combines all payments that have cleared since the previous one, minus fees and any refunds or dispute fees. For example:

- Three card payments of $200, $350 and $450 total $1,000.00.
- Card fees are $6.10 + $10.45 + $13.35 = $29.90 (2.9% + 30¢ each).
- Payout: $970.10.

ACH payments only join a payout after they clear, which can take up to 2 business days on top of the payout time. See Pending payments explained.

## Where do I see my payouts?

Go to **Payments** in the left sidebar and open the **Payouts** tab. Each payout shows its date, amount, the status (In transit or Paid) and the payments it contains, so you can match it with your bank statement. You can export the list to CSV on Pro and Business under **Reports → Payments received**.

## Can I change the schedule or get paid instantly?

No. The schedule is fixed at 2 business days, and instant payouts are not available. You can change the destination account, but not the timing. See Connect your bank account for payouts.

## What if a payout is missing?

First check that:

- It has been more than 2 business days (7 days for the first payout).
- Your payout account shows as verified in **Settings → Payments**.
- The payment was made through Ledgerly. A check or a direct transfer to your own bank never appears in payouts.

If all of that is fine, email support@ledgerly.example with the invoice number and payment date.
