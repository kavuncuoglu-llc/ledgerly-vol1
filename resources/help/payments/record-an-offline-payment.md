# Record an offline payment

If a client paid you by check, cash, or a bank transfer straight to your own account, the invoice does not know about it until you record the payment. Open the invoice and click **Record payment**, or use **Mark as paid** if the invoice was paid in full.

## Why does an invoice still show unpaid?

This is the most common payment question. Ledgerly updates invoices automatically only for payments made through its own payment page. Online payments update the invoice within a minute. A payment made outside Ledgerly, such as a check or a bank transfer to your own account, leaves the invoice as Sent or Overdue, and reminders may continue, until you record it.

## How do I record a payment?

1. Open **Invoices** and click the invoice.
2. Click **Record payment**.
3. Enter the **Amount**. It defaults to the outstanding balance.
4. Choose the **Date** the money was received.
5. Choose the **Method**: card, bank transfer, check, cash or other.
6. Optionally add a reference, such as a check number.
7. Tick **Send receipt to client** if you want one emailed.
8. Click **Save payment**.

The payment appears in the invoice's payment history.

## What is Mark as paid?

**Mark as paid** is a shortcut for the common case. It records a payment for the full outstanding balance, dated today, with the method you pick, and sets the invoice to Paid. Use Record payment instead if the amount, date or method differs.

## How do partial payments work?

Enter a smaller amount than the balance. The invoice stays Sent or Overdue and the outstanding balance is reduced. For example, on a $1,000.00 invoice with a $400.00 check recorded, the outstanding balance becomes $600.00 and the invoice remains open. Record the next payment when it arrives. When the balance reaches zero, the invoice becomes Paid.

Late fees are calculated on the outstanding balance, so a partial payment also reduces future fees.

## Do offline payments cost anything?

No. Ledgerly charges no processing fee for payments you record yourself, because no money moves through Ledgerly. These payments are not part of your payouts and cannot be refunded inside Ledgerly. They do appear in **Reports → Payments received**.

## What if I recorded the wrong amount?

Open the invoice, find the payment in its history, and click **⋯ → Delete payment**, then record it again correctly. The invoice status and balance recalculate automatically. This is only possible for payments you recorded manually, not for online payments.

**Should I tell my client?**

Receipts for online payments are sent automatically. For offline payments, tick **Send receipt to client** so the client has proof. See Payment receipts.
