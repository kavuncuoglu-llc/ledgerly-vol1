# Supported countries and currencies for payments

You can receive payouts if your bank account is in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, Australia, or most EU countries. Invoices can be issued in more than 30 currencies, and the currency is set per client.

## Where can I receive payouts?

Payouts go to a bank account in one of these places:

- United States
- Canada
- United Kingdom
- Australia
- Most countries in the European Union

The country you choose when you connect your payout account must match where the bank account is held. If your country is not on this list, you can still use Ledgerly to create, send and track invoices, and record payments yourself, but you cannot take online payments. See Connect your bank account for payouts.

## Which payment methods work where?

| Method | Availability |
|---|---|
| Card payments | Supported payout countries, from clients paying with most major cards |
| Bank transfer (ACH) | US only: US bank accounts, US dollar invoices |

Clients can live anywhere. The restriction is on your payout country and, for ACH, on the client's bank.

## Which currencies can I invoice in?

Ledgerly supports 30+ currencies, including USD, CAD, GBP, EUR and AUD. The default is USD. Currency is set on the client record, under **Clients → [client] → Edit → Currency**, and every invoice for that client uses it. See Per-client currency and payment terms.

## What currency do I get paid in?

Your payout lands in your bank account's local currency. If an invoice is in a different currency from your bank account, the amount is converted when the payout is made. Check with your bank about any charges on their side. The payment fee percentages stay the same regardless of currency, but the fixed 30¢ in the card fee is applied in the equivalent amount of the invoice currency.

## Can I mix currencies across clients?

Yes. One account can have a US client billed in USD, a UK client billed in GBP and a German client billed in EUR. Reports show totals per currency, so figures in different currencies are never added together.

## Why is a payment option missing for my client?

Check these items:

- Is the invoice in USD and the client in the US? That is required for ACH.
- Is your payout account verified in **Settings → Payments**?
- Is the payment method switched on in **Settings → Payments**?
- Is your account on the Pro or Business plan?

If everything is set correctly, contact support@ledgerly.example with the invoice number and the client's country.

**Can I change my payout country?**

Yes. Open **Settings → Payments → Payout account → Change account** and connect a bank account in the new country. It must be verified before online payments resume.
