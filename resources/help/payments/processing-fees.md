# Processing fees for online payments

Ledgerly charges a processing fee only when a client pays online. Cards cost 2.9% + 30¢ per successful charge, and ACH bank transfers cost 0.8% capped at $5. There are no monthly payment fees on top of your plan price.

## What are the fees?

| Item | Fee |
|---|---|
| Card payment | 2.9% + 30¢ per successful charge |
| Bank transfer (ACH, US only) | 0.8% of the payment, maximum $5 |
| Chargeback (dispute) | $15 per dispute |
| Refund | No extra fee, but the original processing fee is not returned |
| Failed or declined payment | No fee |
| Offline payments you record yourself | No fee |

Plan prices are separate: Pro is $19/month and Business is $49/month, or $190 and $490 per year.

## How are fees taken?

Fees are deducted automatically from each payment before it reaches your payout. Your client always pays the invoice total and never sees the fee. Open **Payments** in the sidebar to see each payment's gross amount, fee and net amount.

## How do I work out the fee on an invoice?

For cards, multiply the amount by 0.029 and add $0.30. For ACH, multiply by 0.008 and cap the result at $5.

- A $1,200.00 card payment: $34.80 + $0.30 = $35.10 fee, so you receive $1,164.90.
- A $1,200.00 ACH payment: 0.8% is $9.60, but the cap applies, so the fee is $5.00 and you receive $1,195.00.

ACH costs less than a card at every amount, and the gap grows once the $5 cap applies.

## Does partial payment change the fee?

Each partial payment is its own charge. A client who pays an invoice in two card payments pays 30¢ twice, so the total fee is slightly higher than a single payment.

## Do I get fees back on refunds?

No. If you refund a payment, in full or in part, the processing fee on the original payment is not returned. See Issue a refund.

## Can I pass fees on to my client?

Ledgerly does not add a surcharge for you. If you want to recover fees, build the cost into your prices, or switch off cards in **Settings → Payments** so that clients pay by ACH or by an offline method. Check the rules on surcharging where you operate.

**Where can I find fee totals for accounting?**

Open **Reports → Payments received**, choose a date range, and export to CSV. The export includes gross amount, fee and net amount for each payment, which you can send to your accountant or an accounting integration on the Business plan.
