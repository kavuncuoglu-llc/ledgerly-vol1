# Bank transfer (ACH) payments

Clients with a US bank account can pay invoices by ACH bank transfer. It costs 0.8% of the payment, capped at $5, which makes it the cheaper option for large invoices.

## What is ACH and who can use it?

ACH is the US electronic bank transfer network. Ledgerly offers it on the payment page when:

- Your account is on the Pro or Business plan.
- **Bank transfer (ACH)** is switched on in **Settings → Payments**.
- The client pays from a US bank account.
- The invoice is in US dollars.

ACH is US only. Clients elsewhere see the card option instead. See Supported countries and currencies for payments.

## How much does ACH cost?

The fee is 0.8% of the amount, with a maximum of $5 per payment.

| Payment | ACH fee (0.8%, max $5) | Card fee (2.9% + 30¢) |
|---|---|---|
| $250.00 | $2.00 | $7.55 |
| $625.00 | $5.00 | $18.43 |
| $5,000.00 | $5.00 | $145.30 |

Any payment of $625 or more pays the $5 maximum. There are no monthly fees.

## How do I turn it on?

1. Open **Settings → Payments**.
2. Make sure a payout account is connected.
3. Switch **Bank transfer (ACH)** on.
4. Click **Save**.

You can switch it off at any time. If you would rather clients pay by card only, leave it off.

## How does the client pay?

On the payment page the client chooses **Bank transfer**, then signs in to their bank securely or enters their routing and account numbers. They confirm the amount and submit. Ledgerly never shows you their bank details.

## Why does the payment show as pending?

ACH is not instant. A payment can show as **pending** for up to 2 business days while the bank clears it. During that time:

- The invoice is not yet marked Paid.
- Reminders may still go out until the payment clears, so consider sending the client a note.
- Once cleared, the invoice updates to Paid and a receipt is emailed.

See Pending payments explained for more detail.

## What if an ACH payment fails?

Occasionally a bank returns a transfer, for example because of insufficient funds or a closed account. The payment is marked Failed, the invoice goes back to its previous status, and you are notified by email. The client can try again with another method from the same payment link.

**Is this the same as a direct transfer to my own bank?**

No. If a client sends money straight to your bank account, outside Ledgerly, the invoice does not know about it. Record it with **Record payment**. See Record an offline payment.
