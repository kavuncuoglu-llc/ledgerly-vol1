# Payment links and the client payment page

Every invoice you email includes a secure payment link. It opens a hosted payment page where the client can review the invoice and pay by card or bank transfer without creating an account.

## What is a payment link?

A payment link is a unique, secure URL for one invoice. Ledgerly adds it to the invoice email as a **Pay now** button and also prints it on the PDF. Anyone with the link can view and pay that invoice, so share it only with your client.

Online payments are available on the Pro and Business plans and need a connected payout account.

## How do I find or copy the link?

1. Open the invoice from **Invoices**.
2. Click **⋯ → Copy payment link**.
3. Paste the link into a message, a chat or your own email.

This is useful when the original email was lost, or when you want to send the link through another channel.

## What does the client see on the payment page?

- Your business name and logo
- The invoice number, issue date and due date
- The line items and the total due, including any late fee as a separate line
- The outstanding balance if earlier payments were made
- The payment methods you have switched on, and a button to pay

Late fees increase the total shown on the payment page, so the client always pays the up-to-date amount. If you waive a late fee, the page updates immediately.

## Which payment methods are shown?

The methods come from **Settings → Payments**, where you can toggle each one:

| Method | Shown when |
|---|---|
| Credit and debit cards | Switched on |
| Bank transfer (ACH) | Switched on, and the invoice is in USD for a US client |

If both are off, or no payout account is connected, the invoice has no payment page and the client must pay another way.

## What happens after the client pays?

The page shows a confirmation, the invoice updates within a minute for cards, a receipt is emailed, and the payment appears in your **Payments** list. ACH payments show as pending until they clear. See Pending payments explained.

## Does the link ever expire?

No. The link stays valid for the life of the invoice. It stops working for payment once the invoice is Paid or Void, and shows a message instead. If you resend the invoice, the same link is used. Clients with several open invoices can also use the Client portal to see them all in one place.

**Can I regenerate a link?**

Not at present. If you think a link was shared with the wrong person, void the invoice and issue a new one, which gets a new link.
