# Connect your bank account for payouts

To receive money from online card and ACH payments, connect a bank account under **Settings → Payments**. Until you do, clients cannot pay your invoices online.

## What do I need before I start?

- A Pro or Business plan, or an active 14-day trial.
- A business or personal bank account in a supported country: the US, Canada, the UK, Australia or most of the EU.
- Your legal name or business name and address.
- The account and routing details for your bank (for example routing and account number in the US, sort code and account number in the UK, or an IBAN in the EU).
- A government ID, if Ledgerly asks you to verify your identity.

## How do I connect the account?

1. Go to **Settings → Payments**.
2. In the **Payout account** section, click **Connect bank account**.
3. Select the country where the bank account is held.
4. Enter your name or business name, address and bank details.
5. Confirm and click **Save and verify**.

The status shows **Verifying** while Ledgerly checks the details. Most accounts are verified within one business day.

## What does verification involve?

Ledgerly confirms that the account exists and that the name matches your details. In some cases you are asked to upload a photo of an ID or a recent bank statement. If something is missing, the status changes to **Action needed** and a banner on the Payments page lists what to provide. Online payments stay switched off until verification completes.

## When will I get my first payout?

The first payout can take up to 7 days after your first successful online payment. After that, payouts arrive within 2 business days. See Payout schedule.

## How do I change my bank account?

1. Open **Settings → Payments → Payout account**.
2. Click **Change account** and enter the new details.
3. Verify it as above.

Payments already in progress are paid to the account that was active when they were taken. Changing the account is limited to the Owner on the Business plan. Admins and Members cannot see full bank details. For security, only the last four digits of the account number are displayed once saved.

## Can I connect more than one account?

No. Each Ledgerly account has one payout account. If you operate several businesses, use a separate Ledgerly account for each.

**What if verification fails?**

Common reasons are a name that does not match the bank record, a mistyped account number, or an unsupported country. Re-enter the details with **Change account**. If it still fails, email support@ledgerly.example with the country and the error shown.
