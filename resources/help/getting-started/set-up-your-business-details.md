# Set up your business details

Your business details appear at the top of every invoice and in the emails your clients receive. Fill them in once under **Settings → Business**, and they apply to all future invoices.

## Which business details can I enter?

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Business name | Shown as the sender on invoices and emails |
| Contact name | The person clients should reply to |
| Business email | Used as the reply-to address on invoice emails |
| Phone | Optional |
| Address | Street, city, region, postal code and country |
| Tax ID | Optional. Shown on invoices when filled in |
| Default currency | Used for new clients; USD unless you change it |

The business name and country are the only required fields. Everything else can stay empty, but invoices look more professional with an address and contact email.

## How do I fill them in?

1. Select **Settings** in the left sidebar.
2. Open **Business**.
3. Enter your details in each field.
4. Select **Save changes**.

A live preview on the right side of the page shows how the header of an invoice will look. Changes apply right away to every Draft invoice. Invoices that were already sent keep the details they were sent with, so past documents never change retroactively.

## What does the Tax ID do?

If you enter a Tax ID, such as a VAT number or EIN, it is printed in the invoice header. Clients with their own tax ID have it shown in the "Bill to" block. Ledgerly does not check whether a tax ID is valid, file taxes or give tax advice. Tax rates are set up separately under **Settings → Taxes**.

## Which email address do clients see?

Invoice emails are sent from `invoices@mail.ledgerly.example`, with your business name as the display name. The business email you enter here is set as the reply-to address, so when a client answers, the message goes to you. Business plan accounts can send from their own domain instead; see the article "Custom sending domain".

## Can I change my details later?

Yes. You can edit the fields at any time. If you move or rename your business, update the details before you create your next invoice. To correct an invoice that was already sent, void it and create a new one, since voided invoice numbers are not reused. If you only need to change the currency for one client, edit that client's record instead of the account default.

After the business details are saved, the next step is to add your logo and branding.
