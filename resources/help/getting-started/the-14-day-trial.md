# The 14-day trial

Every new Ledgerly account gets 14 days of Pro for free, with no card required. When the trial ends, your account moves to the Free plan unless you choose a paid plan.

## What is included in the trial?

During the trial you have everything in the Pro plan:

- Unlimited clients and invoices
- Recurring invoices
- Late fees and automatic reminders
- Online payments
- Custom logo and colors
- CSV export of reports
- Zapier and the Google Calendar reminders feed

The trial does not include Business features such as team seats, API access, accounting integrations or a custom sending domain. To try those, upgrade to Business under **Settings → Billing → Plan**.

## How do I see how many trial days are left?

A banner at the top of the Dashboard shows the days remaining. You can also open **Settings → Billing → Plan**, where the trial end date is shown next to your current plan. The trial counts calendar days from the moment you confirm your email.

## What happens when the trial ends?

If you have not chosen a plan by the end of day 14, your account switches to Free. Nothing is deleted. Features that belong to Pro lock instead:

- Recurring schedules pause and stop generating invoices.
- Late fees and automatic reminders stop running.
- Online payment links stop accepting new payments. Invoices already sent remain visible to clients.
- Ledgerly branding returns to your PDFs.

The Free plan allows up to 3 clients and 5 invoices per calendar month. If you already have more than that, your existing clients and invoices stay available to view, but you cannot add new ones past the limit until you upgrade or the month rolls over.

## How do I keep Pro features after the trial?

1. Open **Settings → Billing → Plan**.
2. Choose **Pro** ($19/month or $190/year) or **Business** ($49/month or $490/year).
3. Choose monthly or annual billing. Annual billing gives you 2 months free.
4. Enter your card details and confirm.

You can upgrade on any day of the trial. Your plan changes immediately, and the paid period starts that day, so unused trial days are not carried over.

## Can I extend or repeat the trial?

The trial runs once per account and cannot be extended or restarted. If you need a little more time to decide, contact support at support@ledgerly.example before the trial ends and explain what you are still evaluating.

Downgrading later takes effect at the end of your billing period, and data is never deleted when you downgrade.
