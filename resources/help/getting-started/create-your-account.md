# Create your account

You can create a Ledgerly account in about two minutes with an email address and password, or with your Google account. No credit card is needed, and every new account starts with a 14-day Pro trial.

## What do I need to sign up?

You need a working email address and one of the following:

- A password of at least 10 characters, or
- A Google account you can sign in with.

You do not need to enter a card number or choose a plan. If you plan to invite colleagues later, note that team seats are part of the Business plan, so the account you create now becomes the Owner account.

## How do I sign up with email and password?

1. Open the Ledgerly sign-up page and choose **Sign up with email**.
2. Enter your name, email address and a password of at least 10 characters.
3. Select **Create account**.
4. Open the confirmation email we send you and select **Confirm my email**.
5. Return to the browser tab. Ledgerly signs you in and starts the short setup guide.

The confirmation email usually arrives within a minute. If you do not see it, check your spam folder, then use **Resend confirmation** on the waiting screen. The link in the email stays valid for 24 hours.

## How do I sign up with Google?

Choose **Continue with Google** on the sign-up page and pick the Google account you want to use. Ledgerly takes your name and email address from Google and does not ask you to set a password. You can add a password later under **Settings → Account → Security**, which lets you sign in either way. See the article "Magic-link sign-in and Google sign-in" for details.

## What happens after I sign up?

The setup guide asks for your business name, country and the currency you usually invoice in. You can skip any question and finish it later under **Settings → Business**. Your country sets the starting time zone and currency defaults, which you can change at any time under **Settings → Account → Your profile**.

When the guide closes you land on the Dashboard, and your 14-day Pro trial begins immediately. The article "The 14-day trial" explains what the trial includes and what happens when it ends.

## Common sign-up problems

| Problem | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "This email is already registered" | Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page, or sign in with Google if you used it before. |
| Confirmation email never arrives | Check spam, then resend. Make sure the address has no typo. |
| Password rejected | Use at least 10 characters. Longer phrases work well. |
| Wrong email address entered | Sign up again with the right address. Unconfirmed accounts are removed after 7 days. |

Each person needs their own email address. One email address can belong to only one Ledgerly account.
