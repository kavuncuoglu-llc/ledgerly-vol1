# Create your first invoice

You can create an invoice in under a minute from **Invoices → New invoice**. Ledgerly saves it as a Draft, so nothing reaches your client until you send it.

## What do I need before I start?

You need at least one client. If you have not added one yet, read "Add your first client" first, or use **Add new client** from inside the invoice form. Your business details should also be filled in under **Settings → Business**.

## How do I create the invoice?

1. Select **New invoice** at the top right of any screen.
2. Choose a client. Their currency and default payment terms fill in automatically.
3. Check the invoice number. Numbers look like INV-4471 and increase automatically.
4. Set the issue date and the due date.
5. Add line items.
6. Add a discount, tax or notes if needed.
7. Select **Save as draft**.

## How do line items work?

Each line item has a description, a quantity, a unit price and an optional tax rate. The line total is calculated for you. An invoice can hold up to 50 line items. Select **Add line** to add another, and drag the handle on the left to reorder them.

Tax rates come from **Settings → Taxes**. You can apply up to 5 tax rates to one line, and each rate can be inclusive or exclusive of the price.

## What about discounts, notes and attachments?

- **Discount**: enter a percentage or a fixed amount. It applies to the whole invoice, not a single line.
- **Notes**: text shown below the totals, for example payment instructions.
- **Attachments**: up to 5 files, 10 MB each. Very large attachments can cause the PDF to fail, so compress big files first.

The currency is set by the client, not the invoice. To invoice in another currency, change it on the client record.

## Why can't I save my invoice?

Ledgerly will not save an invoice below the minimum invoice amount, which is $1.00 by default. You can change this under **Settings → Invoices → Minimum invoice amount**. You will also see an error if a client is missing, a line has no description, or you have gone over the plan limits. On the Free plan you can create up to 5 invoices per calendar month.

## What happens to the draft?

A Draft is visible only to you. Open it from **Invoices**, filter by **Draft**, edit anything, or delete it. The invoice number is reserved while it is a draft. Once you are happy, select **Send**, which is covered in "Send your first invoice". You can also select **Download PDF** to preview exactly what your client will see.

The due date comes from the client's payment terms, which default to Net 30. See "Set default payment terms" to change that.
