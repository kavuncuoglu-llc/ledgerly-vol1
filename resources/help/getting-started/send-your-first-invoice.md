# Send your first invoice

Open a Draft invoice and select **Send**. Ledgerly emails your client the invoice as a PDF with a secure payment link, and the status changes from Draft to Sent.

## How do I send an invoice by email?

1. Open **Invoices** and select the Draft.
2. Review the invoice, or select **Download PDF** to check the layout.
3. Select **Send**.
4. Check the recipients, subject and message. Recipients come from the client's billing contacts, up to 3 addresses.
5. Select **Send invoice**.

The email comes from `invoices@mail.ledgerly.example` with your business name as the sender, and replies go to your business email. Business plan accounts can use their own domain instead. Online payment links appear only when you have connected payments; see "Connect payments".

## What does the client receive?

The client gets one email containing:

- Your message, based on your email message template
- The invoice as a PDF attachment
- A **View and pay invoice** button leading to a secure payment page

On the payment page they can pay by card or, for US bank accounts, by ACH bank transfer. A receipt is emailed to them automatically after every payment.

## What status will my invoice have?

The invoice moves to **Sent** once delivery is accepted. It stays Sent until the due date. The day after the due date, at around 1 a.m. in your account's time zone, an unpaid invoice becomes **Overdue**. When the full amount is received, it becomes **Paid**.

## Can I send it without email?

Yes. Select **Download PDF** and send the file yourself. Then open the invoice and use **⋯ → Mark as sent** so that the due-date tracking starts. You can also copy the payment link from the invoice and share it in a message.

## What if the email is not delivered?

"Not delivered" usually means the client's mailbox rejected the message. Open the invoice and check the **Activity log** for the reason. Common causes are a typo in the address and a full mailbox. Correct the address on the client record and resend.

Other things to know after sending:

- You can send a manual reminder at any time, with a maximum of one per 24 hours per invoice.
- A sent invoice can still be edited if no payment has been recorded, but the client will not see the changes until you resend it.
- To cancel a sent invoice, use **⋯ → Void**. The number is kept for the record and is never reused.
