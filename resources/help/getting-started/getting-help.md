# Getting help

You can reach Ledgerly support through the help center, the in-app chat widget or email. Which options you have depends on your plan.

## What support options do I have?

| Channel | Free | Pro | Business |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Help center search | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Email: support@ledgerly.example | Reply within 2 business days | Reply within 1 business day | Priority, reply within 4 business hours |
| In-app chat widget | No | Reply within 1 business day | Reply within 4 business hours |

Business accounts receive priority support, so their chat and email requests are handled first. Trial accounts get the Pro level of support for the 14 days.

## How do I search the help center?

Use the search box at the top of any help center page, or select the **?** icon in the top bar of the app. Type a few words, such as "late fee" or "export data". Results are ranked by relevance, and each article stays focused on one topic. Articles are grouped into categories like Getting started, Account and Billing, so you can also browse.

## How do I start a chat?

Pro and Business users see a chat bubble at the bottom right of the app.

1. Select the bubble.
2. Describe your problem in a sentence or two.
3. Attach a screenshot if it helps.
4. Send the message.

Replies appear in the same window, and you also get an email copy. If you close the chat, the conversation is kept.

## How do I contact support by email?

Write to support@ledgerly.example from the email address on your account. This lets us find your account without asking for extra details. Include:

- What you were trying to do
- What happened instead, including any error message
- The invoice number or client name, if the issue relates to one
- Your browser and its version

Never include your password, full card number or backup codes in a message. Support will never ask for them.

## What should I check first?

Many questions have a quick answer:

- If an invoice email was "not delivered", open the invoice's **Activity log**.
- If a payment was received but the invoice still shows unpaid, the payment was likely made outside Ledgerly and must be recorded with **Record payment**.
- If a PDF will not download in Safari, check that you are on Safari 16 or later.
- If a feature appears locked, check your plan under **Settings → Billing → Plan**.

## How are urgent problems handled?

If you cannot sign in, or payments appear to be failing for all of your clients, say so in the first line of your message. Support triages these first.
