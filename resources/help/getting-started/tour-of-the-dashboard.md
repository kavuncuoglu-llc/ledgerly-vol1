# Tour of the dashboard

The Dashboard is the first screen you see after signing in. It shows what you are owed, what is overdue and what has been paid recently, and the left sidebar takes you everywhere else.

## What is in the left sidebar?

The sidebar lists seven areas:

| Item | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Dashboard | Summary of your money and recent activity |
| Invoices | All invoices, filterable by status |
| Recurring | Schedules that create invoices automatically (Pro and Business) |
| Clients | Your client list, imports and archived clients |
| Payments | Payments received, payouts and disputes |
| Reports | Revenue, Aging, Client statements, Tax summary, Payments received |
| Settings | Business details, branding, billing, account and team |

## What do the summary cards show?

Four cards sit across the top of the Dashboard:

- **Outstanding**: the total of all Sent and Overdue invoices, minus any partial payments already received.
- **Overdue**: the part of that total past its due date.
- **Paid this month**: payments received since the first of the month.
- **Drafts**: the number of invoices you have started but not sent.

Select a card to jump to the matching list of invoices. Amounts are shown in your default currency. If you invoice in several currencies, each currency appears on its own line.

## What is the revenue chart?

Below the cards, a bar chart shows money received per month for the last 12 months. Hover over a bar to see the exact total. Use the range menu above the chart to switch between this year, last year and a custom range. For deeper figures, open **Reports → Revenue**.

## What is in the activity feed?

The right-hand column lists recent events, newest first: an invoice was sent, viewed or paid, a reminder went out, or an email could not be delivered. Select any entry to open the invoice it refers to. The feed keeps the last 30 days. Older events remain available on each invoice's own Activity log.

## What are the quick actions?

The **New invoice** button at the top right is available on every screen. Next to it, the **+** menu lets you add a client, record a payment or start a recurring schedule. In the top bar you will also find the search box, which finds invoices by number, client name or amount, and your profile menu for **Your profile**, **Billing** and **Sign out**.

If your account is new, a Getting started checklist appears on the Dashboard. It tracks business details, branding, first client, first invoice and connected payments. Dismiss it with **Hide checklist**, and reopen it from the profile menu.
