# Add your logo and branding

You can add your logo, an accent color and footer text so invoices and emails look like they come from your business. Custom logo and colors are available on Pro and Business, including during the 14-day trial.

## What branding options are available?

All branding settings live under **Settings → Branding**.

| Option | What it controls |
| --- | --- |
| Logo | Shown at the top of invoices, PDFs, the payment page and the client portal |
| Accent color | Buttons, headings and table highlights |
| Invoice template | Classic, Modern or Compact layout |
| Invoice footer text | A line at the bottom of every invoice, such as bank details or a thank-you note |
| Email message template | The default text of the email that carries the invoice |

## How do I upload my logo?

1. Open **Settings → Branding**.
2. Under **Logo**, select **Upload logo**.
3. Choose a PNG, JPG or SVG file. The file can be up to 2 MB.
4. Drag the crop frame if you need to adjust it, then select **Save**.

A transparent PNG or an SVG gives the cleanest result on colored headers. A logo at least 400 pixels wide stays sharp on printed PDFs. To replace the logo, upload a new file. To remove it, select **Remove logo**, and invoices will show your business name in text instead.

## How do I choose an accent color and template?

Select the color swatch under **Accent color** and pick a color, or paste a hex code such as `#2F6FED`. Then choose a template:

- **Classic**: traditional layout with a full-width header.
- **Modern**: more white space and a colored title bar.
- **Compact**: smaller type and tighter rows, useful for invoices with many line items.

The preview beside the settings updates immediately. Pick a color with enough contrast against white, because it is used for text in some templates.

## What do I put in the footer and email message?

The footer text is printed on every invoice, so keep it short. Common uses are payment instructions, a registration number, or a reverse-charge note. The email message template is the default body of the email you send with each invoice. You can still edit the message on each invoice before sending.

## What happens on the Free plan?

Free accounts cannot use a custom logo or accent color. Their PDFs carry Ledgerly branding. If you downgrade from Pro, your logo, color and footer are saved but locked, and they return as soon as you upgrade again. Invoices you already sent keep the look they were sent with.

Branding changes apply to Draft invoices and to everything you send from now on.
