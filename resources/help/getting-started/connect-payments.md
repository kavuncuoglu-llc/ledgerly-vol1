# Connect payments

Connecting payments lets your clients pay invoices online by card or bank transfer. You link a bank account for payouts under **Settings → Payments**. Online payments are available on Pro and Business, including during the trial.

## What do I need to connect payments?

- A Pro or Business plan (or an active trial)
- Your business to be in a supported payout country: the US, Canada, the UK, Australia, and most of the EU
- A bank account in your business's or your own name
- Identity and business details, which our payment partner asks for to meet legal requirements

## How do I connect my bank account?

1. Open **Settings → Payments**.
2. Select **Connect bank account**.
3. Follow the steps to confirm your identity and enter your bank details.
4. Return to Ledgerly when the page says **Payments connected**.

Setup normally takes five to ten minutes. If you stop halfway, you can resume from the same page.

## Which payment methods can clients use?

| Method | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Card | 2.9% + 30¢ per successful charge |
| Bank transfer (ACH, US only) | 0.8%, capped at $5 |

There are no monthly fees on top of your plan. In **Settings → Payments** you can toggle each method on or off. If you turn card payments off, clients only see bank transfer, and the reverse.

## When do I get paid?

Payouts go to your connected bank account within 2 business days of a payment. Your first payout can take up to 7 days. ACH payments can show as "pending" for up to 2 business days before they are confirmed. Online payments update the invoice within a minute, so you do not need to record them yourself. Receipts are emailed to the client automatically.

## What about refunds and chargebacks?

You can refund a payment in full or in part within 90 days from the payment's detail page. Processing fees are not returned. If a client disputes a charge with their bank, you will find it under **Dashboard → Payments → Disputes**, where you can respond with evidence. Each dispute carries a $15 fee.

## What if a client pays outside Ledgerly?

A check, cash or a transfer straight to your own account is not detected. Open the invoice and select **Record payment** with the amount, date and method, or use **Mark as paid** for the full amount.

Ledgerly never stores card numbers. Card details go directly to our payment partner.
