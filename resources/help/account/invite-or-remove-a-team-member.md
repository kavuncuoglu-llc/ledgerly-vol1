# Invite or remove a team member

Owners and Admins on the Business plan can invite people and remove them under **Settings → Team**. You can have up to 5 people on the account in total, counting the Owner.

## How do I invite someone?

1. Open **Settings → Team**.
2. Select **Invite team member**.
3. Enter their email address.
4. Choose a role: Admin or Member. See "Team seats and roles" for what each can do.
5. Select **Send invitation**.

The person receives an email with a **Join Ledgerly** button. The invitation is valid for 7 days. They can join with a new password, a magic link or Google, as long as they use the address you invited.

The invitation shows as **Pending** in the team list. It uses up a seat until it is accepted, declined or cancelled.

## What if the invited person already has a Ledgerly account?

They can accept the invitation with their existing sign-in. They then switch between their own account and yours from the account menu in the top bar. Their personal account and your business account stay separate, with separate invoices and settings.

## How do I resend or cancel an invitation?

Find the pending invitation in the list and open its **⋯** menu:

- **Resend invitation** sends a fresh email and restarts the 7-day window.
- **Cancel invitation** voids the link and frees the seat immediately.

Expired invitations remain in the list as **Expired** until you resend or cancel them.

## How do I remove a team member?

1. Open **Settings → Team**.
2. Select **⋯** next to the person.
3. Choose **Remove from team**.
4. Confirm.

Their access ends at once and any session they have open is signed out. The seat becomes free as soon as you confirm, so you can invite someone else straight away.

## What happens to their work?

Nothing they created is deleted. Invoices, clients and payments they entered stay in the account, and the Activity log keeps their name beside each action. Scheduled recurring invoices they set up continue to run. Review **Recurring** after a removal to check that each schedule still matches your plans.

If they had an API key, revoke it under **Settings → Developers** as well. See "API keys".

## Who can remove whom?

| Action | Owner | Admin |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Remove a Member | Yes | Yes |
| Remove an Admin | Yes | No |
| Remove the Owner | Not possible | Not possible |

To step down as Owner, transfer ownership first. A person who is removed can be invited again later in the same way.
