# Export my data

You can download a complete copy of your Ledgerly data at any time from **Settings → Account → Export my data**. The export is a ZIP file with CSV files and PDFs. It is available on every plan, including Free.

## What is included in the export?

| Folder or file | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| clients.csv | All clients, including archived ones, with every field |
| invoices.csv | Every invoice with status, dates, totals and tax |
| line-items.csv | Each line item, linked to its invoice number |
| payments.csv | Payments with date, amount and method |
| recurring.csv | Recurring schedules (Pro and Business) |
| pdfs/ | A PDF of every invoice, including Void invoices |

Attachments you added to invoices are placed in an attachments folder. Your login details and card data are never included, because Ledgerly does not store card numbers.

## How do I request an export?

1. Open **Settings → Account → Export my data**.
2. Select **Request export**.
3. Wait for the email titled "Your Ledgerly export is ready". Small accounts take a few minutes. Large accounts with thousands of PDFs can take up to an hour.
4. Select **Download export** in the email, or return to the same page.

You can leave the page after requesting. Only one export can run at a time.

## How long is the download available?

The download link is valid for 7 days and requires you to be signed in. After that, request a new export. Each export is a snapshot from the moment you requested it, so invoices created later are not added.

## Who can request an export?

Only the Owner can export all data. Admins and Members cannot see this option. If you are on the Business plan and want a colleague to take over the task, transfer ownership first or ask the Owner to share the file securely.

## How is this different from report exports?

Reports have their own CSV export on Pro and Business. A report export contains one report for a date range, such as Revenue or Tax summary. **Export my data** is the whole account, regardless of date, and is meant for backups, moving to another tool, or keeping records before closing the account.

## Should I export before closing my account?

Yes. We recommend downloading an export before you use **Close account**, then checking that the ZIP opens. After closing, data is kept 30 days for recovery and then deleted. See "Close your account".

## What if the export fails?

If the email does not arrive within two hours, check spam and try again. If it still fails, contact support@ledgerly.example with the time you requested it.
