# Close your account

You can close your Ledgerly account at **Settings → Account → Close account**. After you close it, your data is kept for 30 days so you can recover the account, and then it is permanently deleted.

## Who can close an account?

Only the Owner can close it. Admins and Members do not see the option. If you are on the Business plan, closing the account ends access for every team member at the same time.

## What should I do before closing?

1. Download your records with **Settings → Account → Export my data**. See "Export my data".
2. Collect any money still owed to you. Once the account is closed, clients can no longer open payment links or the client portal.
3. Download or save reports you need, such as the Tax summary for the year.
4. Check that no payout is pending in **Payments**. Payouts already in progress still arrive within 2 business days.
5. Tell clients where to send future payments.

## How do I close my account?

1. Open **Settings → Account → Close account**.
2. Read the summary of what will happen.
3. Choose a reason from the list. This is optional.
4. Enter your password, or confirm with a magic link if you have no password.
5. Type CLOSE in the box and select **Close my account**.

If you have a paid plan, the subscription is cancelled when the account closes. Charges already made are not refunded for the unused part of the period, and you will not be billed again.

## What happens during the 30 days?

For 30 days, your account is closed but your data remains in storage:

- You cannot sign in to use the app, and clients cannot pay online.
- Recurring invoices stop running, and no reminders or late fees are sent.
- To recover the account, sign in during that time and select **Reopen my account**. Your invoices, clients and settings come back as they were. Paid plans are not restarted automatically, so you choose a plan again.

After 30 days, your invoices, clients, payments and settings are permanently deleted and cannot be restored, even by support.

## What about client records and emails?

Invoices already emailed to clients stay in their mailboxes. Receipts for payments remain with them too. Ledgerly keeps no copy of your data once the 30 days have passed, apart from minimal records we must retain for legal and accounting reasons.

## Should I close or downgrade?

If you only want to stop paying, downgrade to Free instead. Downgrading takes effect at the end of the billing period, nothing is deleted, and features lock instead. You can keep using up to 3 clients and 5 invoices per month. Close the account only when you are sure you no longer need Ledgerly.
