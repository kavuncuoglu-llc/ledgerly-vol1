# Your profile

Your profile holds your name, email, photo, language and location settings. Open it from **Settings → Account → Your profile**, or from the profile menu in the top bar.

## How do I change my name, email and photo?

Edit the **Name** field to change how you appear in the app and in team activity. Your name is not printed on invoices, because invoices use your business name from **Settings → Business**.

To add a photo, select **Upload photo** and choose a PNG or JPG up to 2 MB. It appears next to your name in the top bar and on team activity. Select **Remove photo** to go back to your initials.

Changing your email address needs confirmation from both the old and new addresses. See "Change your email address" for the steps.

## What is the "Setting your location" section?

**Setting your location** is where you choose three defaults for your account: country, time zone and currency. Ledgerly uses what you pick here. It does not use your device's GPS, and it does not look for places near you. Nothing about your physical position is collected or stored.

1. Open **Settings → Account → Your profile**.
2. Scroll to **Setting your location**.
3. Choose your **Country**.
4. Choose your **Time zone**. The list shows names such as America/Chicago with the current UTC offset.
5. Choose your **Default currency**.
6. Select **Save changes**.

Your country sets suggested defaults when you sign up, and you can change any of the three independently. Currency here is only the default for new clients. Each client keeps its own currency.

## How do I change the language?

Under **Language**, pick the language for the app's menus and screens. Under **Date and number format**, pick the pattern you prefer, such as 31/12/2026 or 12/31/2026. These settings affect only what you see. They do not change how your clients see invoices, which follow your invoice template and message settings.

## How does my time zone affect due dates and overdue checks?

Your time zone controls when a day starts and ends for your account. Two things depend on it:

- **Due dates**: a due date is a calendar date in your time zone. An invoice due on the 15th is not late until the 15th is over where you are.
- **The nightly overdue check**: the day after the due date, at around 1 a.m. in your time zone, an unpaid Sent invoice changes to Overdue. Reminders and late-fee timing follow from that status.

If you move to a new time zone, update it here. Invoices already marked Overdue stay that way. Invoices not yet overdue use the new time zone at the next check.

## Where else can I manage my account?

Security settings, such as your password and two-factor authentication, are on the same Account page. See "Change your password" and "Two-factor authentication".
