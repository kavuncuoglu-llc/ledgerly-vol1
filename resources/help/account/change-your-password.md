# Change your password

You can change your password at any time under **Settings → Account → Security**. Passwords must be at least 10 characters long.

## How do I change my password while signed in?

1. Open **Settings → Account → Security**.
2. Select **Change password**.
3. Enter your current password.
4. Enter your new password twice.
5. Select **Update password**.

You stay signed in on the device you used. Other devices stay signed in unless you also select **Sign out of all other devices** in the same dialog, which we recommend if you changed your password because of a security concern.

## What makes a good password?

The minimum is 10 characters, and longer is better. Ledgerly rejects a new password if it is one of the most commonly used passwords, or if it matches your email address. A phrase of four or five unrelated words is easy to remember and hard to guess. Use a password you do not use anywhere else. A password manager helps with this.

There is no forced expiry, so you do not need to change it on a schedule.

## What if I forgot my password?

1. Go to the sign-in page and select **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your account email address and select **Send reset link**.
3. Open the email and select **Reset password**. The link works for 60 minutes and only once.
4. Choose a new password.

If the email does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder and confirm you used the right address. If you use two-factor authentication, you will still be asked for a code after the reset, so keep your authenticator app or backup codes nearby.

## I signed up with Google. Do I have a password?

Not unless you add one. Accounts created with Google sign-in have no password. Under **Settings → Account → Security** you will see **Set password** instead of **Change password**. Setting one lets you sign in with either method. You can also skip a password and use a magic link, covered in "Magic-link sign-in and Google sign-in".

## Will changing my password affect anything else?

- Open sessions on other devices end only if you chose to sign them out.
- API keys on the Business plan are not affected by a password change.
- Team members have their own passwords. You cannot see or change them.
- Sessions expire after 30 days of inactivity, even without a password change.

## What if I suspect someone else has access?

Change your password at once, sign out of all other devices, turn on two-factor authentication, and review recent activity. Then contact support@ledgerly.example so we can look at the account with you.
