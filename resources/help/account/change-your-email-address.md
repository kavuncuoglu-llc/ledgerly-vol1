# Change your email address

You can change the email address on your Ledgerly account in **Settings → Account → Your profile**. For security, you must confirm the change from both your old and your new address.

## Why do I need to confirm from both addresses?

Your email is how you sign in, reset your password and receive magic links. If someone gained access to your session, they could redirect your account to their own address. Asking the old address to approve the change makes that much harder, and asking the new address proves you can receive mail there.

## How do I change my email?

1. Open **Settings → Account → Your profile**.
2. Next to **Email**, select **Change**.
3. Enter your password. If you have no password, you will be asked to confirm with a magic link instead.
4. Enter the new email address and select **Send confirmation emails**.
5. Open the email sent to your **old** address and select **Approve change**.
6. Open the email sent to your **new** address and select **Confirm new address**.

The change takes effect only after both steps are complete. Both links expire after 24 hours, and until then the old address remains your sign-in email.

## What if I no longer have access to the old address?

You cannot finish the change yourself. Write to support@ledgerly.example from the new address, and tell us your business name, a recent invoice number and your plan. We will confirm your identity and can complete the change manually, which may take a business day or two.

## What changes after the switch?

- You sign in with the new email. Your password stays the same.
- Magic links go to the new address.
- Account notifications go to the new address, unless you set a different one in **Settings → Account → Notifications**.
- Your Google connection is removed if the Google account uses the old email. Reconnect it with a Google account that matches the new address.
- Two-factor authentication and backup codes are unaffected.

Your **business email**, set in **Settings → Business**, is a separate field. It is the reply-to address on invoice emails and does not change when you update your sign-in email. Update it yourself if clients should reply somewhere else.

## Which problems are most common?

| Problem | Fix |
| --- | --- |
| "Email already in use" | That address belongs to another Ledgerly account. Use a different one, or close the other account first. |
| Confirmation not arriving | Check spam folders for both addresses, then select **Resend**. |
| Link says expired | Start the change again. Previous links stop working. |

You can cancel a pending change from the same page with **Cancel change**.
