# Team seats and roles

The Business plan ($49/month or $490/year) includes up to 5 team seats. Each person gets their own sign-in and one of three roles: Owner, Admin or Member. Free and Pro accounts have 1 user.

## How do seats work?

A seat is one person with access to the account. The 5 seats include the Owner, so you can add up to 4 more people. Every person needs their own email address and sign-in. Pending invitations count toward the seat total until accepted, declined or cancelled. Seats are included in the plan price, so there are no per-seat charges.

If you downgrade from Business, team members are locked out at the end of the billing period, but their accounts and the invoices they created are kept. Re-upgrading restores their access.

## What can each role do?

| Capability | Owner | Admin | Member |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Create and send invoices | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Add and edit clients | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Record payments | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| View reports | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Change business details, branding, taxes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Connect payments and view payouts | Yes | Yes | No |
| Invite and remove team members | Yes | Yes (not the Owner) | No |
| Manage API keys and integrations | Yes | Yes | No |
| Change plan or billing details | Yes | No | No |
| Close the account | Yes | No | No |

Every account has exactly one Owner. By default that is the person who created it.

## Can I see what my team has done?

Yes. Each invoice's **Activity log** records who created, edited, sent or voided it. Payments show who recorded them, when they were entered manually.

## How do I change someone's role?

1. Open **Settings → Team**.
2. Find the person in the list.
3. Select the **⋯** menu, then **Change role**.
4. Pick Admin or Member and confirm.

The change takes effect the next time they load a page. Only the Owner and Admins can change roles, and Admins cannot change the Owner's.

## Can I transfer ownership?

Yes. The current Owner can choose **⋯ → Make Owner** next to an Admin on **Settings → Team**. After the Owner confirms with their password, the Admin becomes the Owner and the previous Owner becomes an Admin. This also transfers billing responsibility, so check that the new Owner expects it.

## What about security?

Each team member manages their own password, two-factor authentication and backup codes. We recommend that every team member turns on two-factor authentication. To add or remove people, see "Invite or remove a team member".
