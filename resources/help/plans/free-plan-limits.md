# Free plan limits

The Free plan allows up to 3 clients and 5 invoices per calendar month, for one user. This article explains how the limits are counted and what happens when you reach them.

## What are the Free plan limits?

| Limit | Free plan |
| --- | --- |
| Clients | Up to 3 |
| Invoices | 5 per calendar month |
| Users | 1 |
| Recurring invoices | Not available |
| Late fees | Not available |
| Automatic reminders | Not available |
| Branding | Ledgerly branding on PDFs |

Free is $0 and has no time limit. You can still use reports on screen, PDF client statements, and data export.

## How are clients counted?

Every client record that is not archived counts towards the 3. If you archive a client, the record is hidden but its history is kept, and it stops counting, which frees a slot. Deleting a client is possible only when they have no invoices.

## How are invoices counted?

The count resets at the start of each calendar month, in your account time zone. Every invoice you create in the month counts, including drafts. Voiding an invoice does not give the slot back, so create invoices carefully. The Dashboard shows how many you have used, such as "3 of 5 invoices this month".

## What happens when I reach the limit?

You can still open, send, edit and collect payment on existing invoices, but the **New invoice** or **New client** button shows an upgrade message. Nothing is deleted or locked. At the start of the next month you can create 5 more invoices.

If your account has more than 3 clients, for example after a Pro trial or downgrade, you keep every record. You cannot add new clients until you are back under 3 by archiving some.

## Which limits apply on every plan?

Some limits are the same on all plans, including Pro and Business:

- 50 line items per invoice
- Up to 5 attachments per invoice, each up to 10 MB
- Client CSV import of up to 1,000 rows at a time
- Up to 3 billing contacts per client
- Up to 5 tax rates on one invoice line

These are not changed by upgrading.

## How do I remove the limits?

Upgrade to Pro for $19 per month or $190 per year. Pro has unlimited clients and invoices, plus recurring invoices, late fees, automatic reminders, online payments and CSV export. Every new account gets a 14-day Pro trial first. See Upgrading your plan and Comparing Free, Pro and Business plans.
