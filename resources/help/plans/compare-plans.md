# Comparing Free, Pro and Business plans

Ledgerly has three plans: Free, Pro and Business. Free costs $0, Pro costs $19 per month and Business costs $49 per month. All plans can be billed monthly or annually.

## What does each plan cost?

| Plan | Monthly | Annual |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | $0 | $0 |
| Pro | $19 | $190 |
| Business | $49 | $490 |

Annual billing gives you 2 months free compared with paying monthly. There are no extra monthly fees for online payments. Card payments cost 2.9% + 30¢ and ACH bank transfers cost 0.8%, capped at $5, on every plan.

## What is included in Free?

Free is for people who invoice occasionally. You get up to 3 clients and 5 invoices per calendar month, 1 user, PDF client statements, and all reports on screen. Ledgerly branding appears on your PDFs. Free does not include recurring invoices, late fees or automatic reminders. See Free plan limits for details.

## What does Pro add?

Pro is for solo freelancers and small businesses. It includes everything in Free, plus:

- Unlimited clients and invoices
- Recurring invoices
- Late fees
- Automatic reminders
- Online payments
- Custom logo and colors
- CSV export
- Zapier and the Google Calendar reminders feed

Pro is still 1 user.

## What does Business add?

Business is for teams. It includes everything in Pro, plus:

- Up to 5 team seats
- Roles: Owner, Admin and Member
- API access (60 requests per minute)
- Accounting integrations: QuickBooks Online and Xero
- Custom sending domain, so email comes from your own domain
- Priority support, with chat replies within 4 business hours

## How do I choose?

Use this quick guide:

1. If you send fewer than 5 invoices a month to 3 or fewer clients, Free is enough.
2. If you need recurring invoices, late fees, reminders or online payments, choose Pro.
3. If more than one person works in Ledgerly, or you need QuickBooks, Xero or the API, choose Business.

Every new account starts with a 14-day Pro trial, so you can test Pro features before paying. See Starting your 14-day trial.

## Where do I see or change my plan?

Open **Settings → Subscription**. It shows your current plan, billing interval, next billing date and the **Change plan** button. To move up, see Upgrading your plan. To move down, see Downgrading your plan, which explains what locks.
