# Canceling your subscription

Canceling your paid subscription stops future charges. Your account moves to the Free plan at the end of the period you have already paid for, and none of your data is deleted.

## How do I cancel?

1. Sign in as the account owner and open **Settings → Subscription**.
2. Click **Cancel subscription**.
3. Choose a reason from the list (optional).
4. Read the summary of what will lock.
5. Click **Confirm cancellation**.

You will see "Ends on" followed by a date, and a confirmation email is sent. Only the account Owner can cancel. On Business, Admins and Members do not see the button.

## What happens after I cancel?

Until the end date, you keep every feature you paid for. If you pay monthly, that is the end of the current month's period. If you pay annually, it is the end of the year you paid for. On that date, the account becomes Free:

- Up to 3 clients and 5 invoices per calendar month
- No recurring invoices, late fees or automatic reminders
- No online payments or CSV export
- 1 user, with Ledgerly branding on PDFs

Existing invoices, clients and payment history remain visible. See Downgrading your plan for the full list of what locks. Canceling does not trigger a refund, because you keep access until the end of the paid period, and no further charges are made after that date.

## Can I change my mind?

Yes. Before the end date, open **Settings → Subscription** and click **Resume subscription**. Your plan continues as before and billing carries on at the usual date. After the end date, you can upgrade again at any time and your data is still there. See Upgrading your plan.

## What is the difference between canceling and closing my account?

Cancelling only stops paying. You can keep using Ledgerly on the Free plan. Closing your account, at **Settings → Account → Close account**, shuts it down. Your data is kept for 30 days in case you change your mind, and then it is deleted.

| Action | Result | Data |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cancel subscription | Moves to Free at end of period | Kept |
| Downgrade | Moves to a lower plan at end of period | Kept |
| Close account | Account shut down | Deleted after 30 days |

## Should I export my data first?

It is not required to cancel, but it is a good idea if you plan to stop using Ledgerly. Go to **Settings → Account → Export my data** to download a ZIP of CSV files and PDFs. See Exporting all your data.

## What about open invoices?

Invoices that clients have not yet paid remain open. After you move to Free, online payment links stop accepting new payments, so ask clients to pay another way and record those with **Record payment**.
