# Annual billing

Annual billing charges you once a year and gives you 2 months free compared with paying monthly. Pro costs $190 per year instead of $228, and Business costs $490 per year instead of $588.

## How much does annual billing save?

| Plan | Monthly total for 12 months | Annual price | You save |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pro | $228 | $190 | $38 |
| Business | $588 | $490 | $98 |

The saving is equal to 2 months. There are no other differences between monthly and annual plans. The features, limits and support are the same.

## How do I switch to annual billing?

1. Open **Settings → Subscription**.
2. Click **Change billing interval**.
3. Select **Annual**.
4. Review the amount and the new renewal date.
5. Click **Confirm**.

You are charged right away. Your annual year starts on the day you switch, and the unused part of your current monthly period is credited against the annual price. The credit is shown before you confirm.

## Can I switch back to monthly?

Yes, but the change happens at the end of the annual term. Open the same screen, choose **Monthly**, and the switch is scheduled for your renewal date. You will not be refunded for the unused months of the year you have paid for, and you keep all features until the term ends.

## When does annual billing renew?

An annual plan renews automatically one year after it started, using the card on file. Ledgerly emails the account owner 14 days before renewal, so you have time to switch, downgrade or cancel. The email shows the amount and date.

If the renewal charge fails, Ledgerly retries a few times over the next week and emails you each time. If it still fails, the account moves to Free at the end of the grace period. Your data stays intact. Update your card at **Settings → Subscription → Payment method** to stop this.

## What about changing plan during an annual term?

- **Upgrading** (Pro to Business) takes effect immediately, with credit for the unused part of the year. See Upgrading your plan.
- **Downgrading** takes effect at the end of the annual term. See Downgrading your plan.
- **Canceling** keeps your access until the term ends. See Canceling your subscription.

## Do I get a receipt for annual payments?

Yes. Every payment produces a Ledgerly invoice and receipt, found in **Settings → Subscription → Billing history**. For accounting, the amount shown is the full annual price. See Ledgerly's own invoices and receipts.
