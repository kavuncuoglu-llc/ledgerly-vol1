# Ledgerly's own invoices and receipts

When you pay for Pro or Business, Ledgerly issues you an invoice and a receipt for every charge. You can download them any time from **Settings → Subscription → Billing history**.

## Where do I find my subscription invoices?

1. Open **Settings → Subscription**.
2. Scroll to **Billing history**.
3. Find the charge by date or amount.
4. Click **Invoice** or **Receipt** to download the PDF.

Each row shows the date, plan, billing interval (monthly or annual), amount and status (Paid or Failed). The list goes back to the day you first paid.

## What is the difference between the invoice and the receipt?

The **invoice** is the bill for the plan period, with a Ledgerly invoice number, the plan name, the period covered and the amount. The **receipt** confirms that the payment was received, and shows the card type, last four digits and the payment date. Ledgerly never stores your full card number.

Both are emailed to the account owner automatically when a charge succeeds. Free accounts and trial accounts have no charges, so nothing is issued.

## Can I change the details on them?

Yes, before the charge. Open **Settings → Subscription → Billing details** and edit the:

- Company name
- Billing address
- Billing email (up to 3 addresses can receive receipts)
- Tax ID

Changes apply to future invoices. Invoices already issued are not rewritten, so update your details before upgrading or renewing if they matter for your accounts. Ledgerly does not give tax advice, so ask your accountant what your records need.

## What currency are the charges in?

Subscriptions are charged in US dollars, matching the prices on the plans page: Pro at $19 per month or $190 per year, and Business at $49 per month or $490 per year. Your bank may convert the amount if your card is in another currency, and may add its own fee. Ledgerly does not control that.

## How is this different from invoices I send to clients?

The invoices you create in the **Invoices** section are yours and go to your clients. The ones in **Billing history** are Ledgerly's invoices to you. They never mix, and subscription charges do not appear in your Revenue report. For the invoices you send, see the invoice articles.

## What if I can't find a receipt or it shows Failed?

A Failed row means the card was declined. No money was taken, and no receipt is issued. Update your card at **Settings → Subscription → Payment method**, then click **Retry payment**. If a successful charge has no receipt in the list after an hour, contact support at support@ledgerly.example. See Contacting support.
