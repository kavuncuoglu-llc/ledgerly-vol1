# Downgrading your plan

You can move to a lower plan at any time. The downgrade takes effect at the end of your current billing period, and no data is ever deleted. Features that are not part of the lower plan lock instead.

## How do I downgrade?

1. Open **Settings → Subscription**.
2. Click **Change plan**.
3. Choose the lower plan: Pro or Free.
4. Review the list of features that will lock.
5. Click **Schedule downgrade**.

Subscription shows "Changes to Free on 14 June", for example. Until that date you keep every feature you paid for. To undo it, open the same screen and click **Keep my plan** before the date.

## What locks when I go from Pro to Free?

| Feature | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Recurring invoices | Schedules pause, no new invoices are created |
| Late fees | Stop being applied; existing fee lines stay |
| Automatic reminders | Stop; manual reminders are still possible |
| Online payments | Payment links stop accepting new payments |
| CSV export | Hidden |
| Custom logo and colors | Ledgerly branding returns to PDFs |
| Clients and invoices | Existing ones stay; limit is 3 clients, 5 invoices per month |

Existing invoices, clients and payment history stay visible. If you have more than 3 clients, you can view and search them all but cannot add new ones until you are under the limit.

## What locks when I go from Business to Pro?

- Team members beyond the Owner lose access, and their roles (Admin, Member) are paused. Their history is kept.
- API keys stop working.
- QuickBooks Online and Xero connections pause.
- The custom sending domain is removed and email returns to `invoices@mail.ledgerly.example`.
- Priority support changes to standard Pro support.

Zapier and Google Calendar reminders stay, since they are part of Pro. If you upgrade again, team members and integrations return as they were.

## What should I do before downgrading?

- Download an export if you want a copy, at **Settings → Account → Export my data**. See Exporting all your data.
- Collect payments on open invoices, because online payment links stop working on Free.
- Tell team members they will lose access.

## Will I get a refund?

Because the change happens at the end of the period you have already paid for, no refund is needed. You keep full access until then. For annual plans, the downgrade takes effect at the end of the year.

If you would rather stop paying and not use Ledgerly at all, see Canceling your subscription.
