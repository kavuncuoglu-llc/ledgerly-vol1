# Starting your 14-day trial

Every new Ledgerly account starts with a 14-day Pro trial. You do not need to enter a card, and nothing is charged when the trial ends.

## How do I get the trial?

The trial begins automatically when you sign up. There is nothing to switch on. After you create your account, the top of the Dashboard shows a banner such as "Pro trial: 12 days left", and **Settings → Subscription** shows your plan as "Pro (trial)".

Each account gets one trial. Closing and recreating an account with the same email does not give a second one.

## What can I use during the trial?

You have full Pro access, which means:

- Unlimited clients and invoices
- Recurring invoices
- Late fees and automatic reminders
- Online payments by card and ACH bank transfer
- Custom logo and colors
- CSV export

You do not get Business features such as extra team seats, API access or accounting integrations. The trial is for 1 user.

## What happens when the 14 days end?

If you do nothing, your account moves to the Free plan automatically at the end of day 14. You are not asked for a card and you are not charged. Your data stays exactly as it was. Features that are not part of Free lock instead of being deleted:

1. Recurring schedules pause and stop creating invoices.
2. Late fees and automatic reminders stop.
3. CSV export is hidden.
4. Online payments are turned off for new invoices.
5. Ledgerly branding returns to PDFs.

Free allows 3 clients and 5 invoices per calendar month. If you added more during the trial, existing records stay visible, but you cannot add new clients or invoices beyond the limit. See Free plan limits.

## Can I keep Pro when the trial ends?

Yes. At any time during the trial, open **Settings → Subscription → Change plan**, choose Pro, pick monthly ($19) or annual ($190), and enter a card. Your trial days are not lost. Billing starts when the trial would have ended, so you do not pay twice for the same period. See Upgrading your plan.

You can also choose Business, which starts billing immediately at $49 per month or $490 per year.

## What if I want to extend the trial?

Trials cannot be extended. If you need more time to decide, contact support at support@ledgerly.example. See Contacting support.

## Does the trial count my invoices?

Invoices you create during the trial are real invoices, not samples. They can be sent to clients and paid online. If you only want to test, create a client with your own email address and send yourself an invoice. Void it afterwards. The number of a voided invoice is not reused.
