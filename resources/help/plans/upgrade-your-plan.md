# Upgrading your plan

You can upgrade from Free to Pro or Business, or from Pro to Business, at any time. The new features unlock as soon as payment succeeds.

## How do I upgrade?

1. Open **Settings → Subscription**.
2. Click **Change plan**.
3. Choose **Pro** ($19/month or $190/year) or **Business** ($49/month or $490/year).
4. Select **Monthly** or **Annual** billing.
5. Enter your card details, or confirm the saved card.
6. Click **Upgrade now**.

You will see a confirmation, and a receipt is emailed to the account owner. Ledgerly accepts card payments for subscriptions.

## When does the upgrade take effect?

Immediately. Pro features such as recurring invoices, late fees, reminders and online payments are available within a minute. Business features such as team seats and the API appear straight away as well.

Locked features come back as they were. If you were previously on Pro and downgraded, your old recurring schedules are still there, paused. You must resume them under **Recurring**.

## How much will I be charged?

When you upgrade from Free, the first charge happens now and your billing date is today. If you upgrade during the trial, billing starts when the trial would have ended.

When you upgrade from Pro to Business partway through a billing period, you are charged the Business price for the new period starting today. The unused part of your Pro period is credited against that charge, so you pay only the difference. The credit and the final amount are shown before you confirm.

| From | To | Charge today |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | Pro | $19 monthly or $190 annual |
| Free | Business | $49 monthly or $490 annual |
| Pro | Business | Business price, less credit for unused Pro time |

## Can I switch between monthly and annual when upgrading?

Yes. The billing interval is chosen on the same screen. Annual billing gives you 2 months free. To change interval without changing plan, see Annual billing.

## Do I need to set anything up after upgrading?

Some features need a few settings:

- **Late fees**: turn them on under **Settings → Billing → Late fees**.
- **Reminders**: review the schedule under **Settings → Billing → Reminders**.
- **Online payments**: connect your bank account and check the methods in **Settings → Payments**.
- **Team**: invite people at **Settings → Team** (Business only).

## What if the payment fails?

If the card is declined, the upgrade does not go through and your plan stays the same. Try another card, or check with your bank. If the problem continues, contact support. See Contacting support.

After an upgrade, a Ledgerly invoice for the subscription is available in **Settings → Subscription → Billing history**. See Ledgerly's own invoices and receipts.
