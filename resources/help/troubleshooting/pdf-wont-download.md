# PDF won't download or export

If an invoice PDF or a PDF statement will not download, the cause is usually a very large attachment, a blocked pop-up, or an older browser. Work through the checks below in order.

## Why does a PDF fail to generate?

Ledgerly builds the PDF when you ask for it. The most common failure is an invoice with very large attachments. PDFs fail on very large attachments, so if you attached several big files, try the steps below.

1. Open the invoice and check **Attachments**. You can attach up to 5 files, 10 MB each.
2. Remove the biggest file, or replace it with a smaller version.
3. Click **Download PDF** again.

Invoices with the maximum 50 line items can take a few seconds longer. Wait up to 30 seconds before trying again.

## Does PDF export work in Safari?

Yes, but you need Safari 16 or newer. PDF export works in Safari 16+, and in older versions the download may do nothing or open a blank tab.

If you use Safari and the PDF does not download:

1. Check your version in **Safari → About Safari**. Update macOS or iOS if it is older than 16.
2. Open **Safari → Settings → Websites → Pop-up Windows**, find Ledgerly, and set it to **Allow**.
3. Open **Safari → Settings → General** and make sure the downloads location is a folder you can access.
4. On iPhone or iPad, tap the **aA** icon in the address bar, choose **Website Settings**, and allow downloads.

If you cannot update, use a current version of Chrome, Edge or Firefox for the download.

## What if the PDF downloads but won't open?

Try opening it in a different viewer. A file that opens blank in your browser's built-in viewer often opens fine in Preview, Adobe Acrobat Reader or your phone's Files app. Also check the file size. A file of 0 KB means the download was interrupted, so repeat it.

## Why can't I export a report as PDF or CSV?

Client statements are PDFs on every plan. Report CSV export is available on Pro and Business only. If the **Export CSV** button is marked Pro, you are on Free. See Exporting reports to CSV. A report with a very wide date range can also take longer, so try a shorter range.

## What else can I try?

- Disable browser extensions that block downloads or pop-ups, then try again.
- Try a private window to rule out cached data.
- Sign out and back in, then repeat the download.
- Email the invoice to yourself with **Send**, and open the PDF from your inbox.

If none of this helps, contact support at support@ledgerly.example with your browser and version and the invoice number. See Contacting support.
