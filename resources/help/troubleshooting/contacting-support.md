# Contacting support and response times

You can reach Ledgerly support by email on every plan, and by in-app chat on Pro and Business. Response times depend on your plan.

## How can I contact support?

There are three ways:

1. **Help center search.** Search the help center first, since most questions already have an answer.
2. **In-app chat widget.** Available on Pro and Business. Click the chat button at the bottom right of any page.
3. **Email.** Write to support@ledgerly.example. This is available on all plans, including Free.

Send email from the address on your Ledgerly account, so support can find your account quickly.

## How fast will I get a reply?

| Plan | Chat | Email |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | Not available | Within 2 business days |
| Pro | Within 1 business day | Within 2 business days |
| Business | Within 4 business hours (priority support) | Within 4 business hours |

Business days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays. These are reply times, not resolution times. A first reply may ask for more details.

## What should I include in my message?

A good message gets a faster answer. Include:

- The invoice number, such as INV-4471, or the client name
- What you expected to happen and what happened instead
- The exact text of any error message
- Your browser and its version, if the problem is on screen
- Screenshots, if possible

Never send your password, full card numbers or backup codes. Support will never ask for them. Ledgerly does not store card numbers.

## What can support help with?

Support can look at delivery problems, payments that did not update, billing questions, account access and bugs. For access problems, support will confirm your identity first. Support cannot give tax or legal advice, and cannot recover an invoice you voided, though voided invoices are kept for the record.

Before writing in, these articles solve many common problems:

- Invoice email not delivered
- Payment received but invoice still shows unpaid
- Pending ACH payments
- Late fee charged incorrectly

## How do I check if there is a known issue?

If many pages fail to load or payments seem stuck for everyone, check your email for announcements from Ledgerly. If you do not hear anything, contact support and mention that others may be affected.

## What if I don't get a reply?

First check your spam folder, since replies come from the ledgerly.example domain. If your plan's reply window has passed, reply to your original message instead of starting a new one. This keeps the history together and puts you back at the top of the queue.
