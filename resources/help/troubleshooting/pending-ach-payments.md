# Pending ACH payments

A bank transfer (ACH) payment can show as Pending for up to 2 business days. This is normal, because the US banking network takes time to confirm the transfer. ACH is available for US clients only.

## Why is my ACH payment pending?

Unlike a card, an ACH transfer is not confirmed instantly. After your client submits the payment on the payment page, the transfer is sent to their bank, which approves or rejects it. Until that finishes, the payment is labelled **Pending**.

Weekends and bank holidays do not count as business days. A payment made on a Friday afternoon may clear on Tuesday.

## What does the invoice show while it is pending?

The invoice still shows Sent or Overdue, because the money has not arrived. In **Payments** and in the Payments received report the payment appears with the status **Pending**. Reminders and late fees follow the normal rules, so a client who pays by ACH shortly before the due date is not penalised if the payment later clears.

Your client receives an email receipt right away to confirm the payment was submitted.

## How do I check the status?

1. Click **Payments** in the sidebar.
2. Find the payment by client name or invoice number.
3. Read the **Status** column: Pending, Completed or Failed.

When the payment clears, the status changes to Completed and the invoice moves to Paid automatically, without you doing anything. If the amount was only part of the balance, the invoice stays Sent or Overdue with a lower balance.

## What if the status becomes Failed?

A payment fails when the client's bank rejects it, for example because of insufficient funds, a closed account or incorrect bank details. When this happens:

- The invoice returns to its previous unpaid status.
- You and the client are both emailed.
- The client can try again from the payment link, using the same or a different method.

You do not need to record anything manually.

## How much does ACH cost and when do I get paid?

ACH costs 0.8%, capped at $5 per payment. Once the payment is Completed, payouts go to your connected bank account within 2 business days. Your first payout can take up to 7 days.

| Stage | Typical time |
| --- | --- |
| Client submits payment | Instant |
| Pending until it clears | Up to 2 business days |
| Payout to your bank | Within 2 business days after clearing |

## Should I mark a pending invoice as paid myself?

No. Doing so records a second payment and could double-count it in reports. Wait for the status to change. If a payment has been Pending for more than 2 business days, contact support at support@ledgerly.example with the invoice number. See Contacting support. For payments made outside Ledgerly, see Payment received but invoice still shows unpaid.
