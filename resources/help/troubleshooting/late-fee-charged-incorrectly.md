# Late fee charged incorrectly

If a client was charged a late fee that looks wrong, check the invoice's due date, the late fee setting and whether the invoice was disputed or on a payment plan. You can waive the fee on any single invoice.

## How is a late fee supposed to work?

Late fees are available on Pro and Business. The default fee is 1.5% of the outstanding balance per month, prorated by day, and it can be set between 0.5% and 10% in **Settings → Billing → Late fees**.

A fee is first applied 30 days after the due date, and then accrues daily until the invoice is paid. It is calculated on the invoice total without any earlier late fees, and it never exceeds 25% of the invoice total. Clients see the fee as a separate line on the invoice and in the payment page total.

## What are the common reasons a fee looks wrong?

| Situation | Explanation |
| --- | --- |
| Fee seems too high | The percentage in Settings is higher than you remember |
| Fee appeared early | Count 30 days from the due date, not the invoice date |
| Fee charged on a recurring invoice | Recurring invoices inherit the account setting |
| Fee kept running after a dispute | The invoice was not marked Disputed |
| Fee applied after partial payment | The fee uses the remaining balance |

Open the invoice and compare the due date with today's date to check the timing.

## How do I waive a late fee?

1. Open the invoice.
2. Click **⋯**.
3. Choose **Waive late fee**.
4. Confirm.

Waiving is permanent for that invoice, so no further fees will be added to it, and the fee line is removed from what the client owes. It does not change other invoices.

## How do I stop fees while we sort it out?

Use **⋯ → Mark as disputed**. This pauses reminders and late fees. When you clear the flag, late fees resume from that day with no back-charging of the paused time. A payment plan also pauses fees while installments stay on track. A missed installment by more than 7 days cancels the plan and normal rules resume.

## What about recurring invoices?

Recurring invoices inherit the account's late-fee setting. To change it for one schedule, open **Recurring → [schedule] → Late fee** and choose Use account default, Custom percent or Off. The override applies only to future invoices, not to ones already generated, so for those you need to waive the fee on each invoice.

## Still looks wrong?

If the fee does not match these rules, contact support at support@ledgerly.example with the invoice number, the amount you expected and the amount charged. See Contacting support.
