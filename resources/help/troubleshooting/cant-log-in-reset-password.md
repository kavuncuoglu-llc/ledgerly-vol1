# Can't log in or reset my password

You can sign in to Ledgerly with an email and password, a magic link or Google. If one method fails, you can usually get back in with another or by resetting your password.

## How do I reset my password?

1. On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password**.
2. Enter the email address on your account.
3. Open the email titled "Reset your Ledgerly password".
4. Click the link and choose a new password.

Passwords must be at least 10 characters long. The link works once and expires after 1 hour. If you do not see the email, check your spam folder and make sure you typed the same address you signed up with.

## Can I sign in without a password?

Yes. Click **Email me a sign-in link** on the sign-in page. Ledgerly emails you a magic link that signs you in. If you originally signed up with Google, click **Continue with Google** instead. Using a different Google account from the one you registered with creates an error, so choose the right one.

Clients do not need a password to see their invoices. They use a magic-link email to open the client portal.

## What if I lost access to two-factor authentication?

If two-factor authentication is on, you need a code from your authenticator app or SMS after your password.

1. On the code screen, click **Use a backup code**.
2. Enter one of your 10 single-use backup codes.
3. Once signed in, open **Settings → Account → Security** and set up two-factor authentication again.

Each backup code works once. If you have no backup codes and no access to your device, contact support. They will verify your identity before changing anything. See Contacting support.

## Why was I signed out?

Sessions expire after 30 days of inactivity. Signing in again is all you need to do. Your data is not affected.

## What if I changed my email address?

Changing your email requires confirming from both the old and the new address. Until both are confirmed, the old address stays active, and you must sign in with it. Check both inboxes for the confirmation emails.

## Still can't sign in?

Contact support at support@ledgerly.example from the email address on the account, and describe the error message you see. Free accounts receive a reply within 2 business days. Pro accounts get a reply within 1 business day and Business within 4 business hours. See Contacting support for details.
