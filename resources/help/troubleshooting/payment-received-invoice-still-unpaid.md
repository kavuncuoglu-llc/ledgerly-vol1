# Payment received but invoice still shows unpaid

If the money reached you but the invoice is still Sent or Overdue, the payment was most likely made outside Ledgerly. Payments made outside Ledgerly must be recorded manually. Online payments made through the payment page update the invoice on their own, within a minute.

## Why does the invoice not change to Paid?

Ledgerly only knows about a payment when it passes through the payment page, or when you record it. These payments are invisible to Ledgerly until you tell it:

- A check or cash handed to you
- A bank transfer sent straight to your own bank account
- Money paid through another app or service

In all these cases the invoice keeps its status, and reminders and late fees continue to follow the normal rules until you record the payment.

## How do I record a payment?

1. Open the invoice from **Invoices**.
2. Click **Record payment**.
3. Enter the **amount**, the **date** you received it and the **method**: card, bank transfer, check, cash or other.
4. Click **Save**.

If the client paid the full amount, you can use the shortcut **Mark as paid**, which fills in the outstanding balance for you. The invoice moves to Paid, reminders stop, and the payment appears in the Payments received report.

## What if the client paid only part of the invoice?

Record the amount you received. A partial payment keeps the invoice Sent or Overdue and lowers the outstanding balance. The invoice becomes Paid when the balance reaches zero. Late fees are calculated on the remaining balance.

## How do I check an online payment that has not updated?

Online payments update the invoice within a minute. If more than a few minutes have passed:

1. Open **Payments** in the sidebar and look for the payment.
2. If the status is **Pending**, it is an ACH bank transfer still clearing. See Pending ACH payments.
3. If there is no payment at all, ask the client for their receipt. Receipts are emailed to the client automatically on every payment.
4. Refresh the page, as it may be showing a cached version.

If the payment shows as Completed in Payments but the invoice is still unpaid, contact support with the invoice number. See Contacting support.

## How can I avoid this in the future?

- Record offline payments the day you receive them.
- Turn on online payment methods in **Settings → Payments**, so clients can pay on the payment page. Online payments need Pro or Business.
- Tell clients to use the payment link in the invoice email, rather than paying you directly.

## What if I recorded a payment by mistake?

Open the invoice, find the payment in its history, and click **Delete payment**. The invoice returns to Sent or Overdue and the balance is restored. Online card payments cannot be deleted, and should be refunded instead.
