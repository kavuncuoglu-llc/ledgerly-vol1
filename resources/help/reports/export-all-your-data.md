# Exporting all your data

You can download a complete copy of your Ledgerly account as a ZIP file at **Settings → Account → Export my data**. This is available on every plan, including Free, and your data is never held back.

## What is included in the export?

The ZIP contains CSV files and PDFs:

- **CSV files** for invoices, line items, clients, payments, tax rates, recurring schedules and the activity log.
- **PDFs** of every invoice and, where one was issued, its receipt.
- Attachments you uploaded to invoices (up to 5 files per invoice, 10 MB each).

Sensitive details are never included. Ledgerly does not store card numbers, so they cannot be exported, and your password is not part of the file.

## How do I request an export?

1. Open **Settings → Account**.
2. Scroll to **Export my data**.
3. Click **Request export**.
4. Wait for the email titled "Your Ledgerly export is ready".
5. Click the link in the email and sign in to download the ZIP.

Small accounts are ready within a few minutes. Accounts with many invoices can take up to an hour. The download link is valid for 7 days and works only for the signed-in account owner.

## Who can request it?

On Free and Pro, the single user is the account owner and can export. On Business, only the Owner role can request an export. Admins and Members do not see the **Export my data** button.

## How is it different from a report export?

A report export, described in Exporting reports to CSV, gives one table for one date range. The full export is meant for backups, moving to another tool, or keeping records after you leave. It includes everything regardless of dates.

| Need | Use |
| --- | --- |
| One quarter's payments | Payments received report, Export CSV |
| A backup before closing | Export my data |
| Records for an accountant | Export my data or Tax summary CSV |

## What should I do before closing my account?

Download an export first. When you close the account at **Settings → Account → Close account**, your data is kept for 30 days so you can recover it, and then it is deleted. Downgrading never deletes data, so there is no need to export before a plan change, though many people do it as a precaution.

## Can I schedule regular backups?

There is no automatic full export. Daily backups of your data are kept by Ledgerly for 35 days for disaster recovery, but they are not available for download. If you want your own regular copy, request an export on a schedule that suits you, such as monthly. You can keep older files wherever you store your records.
