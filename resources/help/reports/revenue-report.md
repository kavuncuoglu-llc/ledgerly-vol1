# Revenue report

The Revenue report shows how much you invoiced and how much you collected over a period. Open it from **Reports → Revenue**.

## What does the Revenue report show?

At the top you see three totals for the selected date range:

- **Invoiced**: the total of all invoices dated in the range, including tax and any late fees.
- **Collected**: payments received in the range.
- **Outstanding**: the unpaid balance on those invoices today.

Below the totals is a chart with one bar per day, week or month, depending on the length of the range. Under the chart a table lists revenue by client, sorted from highest to lowest, with the number of invoices for each.

## Which invoices are included?

The report counts invoices with status Sent, Overdue and Paid. Draft invoices are not counted because they have not been sent. Void invoices are excluded because they were cancelled and are kept only for the record.

Partial payments count towards Collected as soon as they are recorded, while the invoice stays Sent or Overdue and its unpaid balance appears under Outstanding.

If a client is billed in a different currency, their amounts are shown in that currency. The report groups totals by currency and does not convert between them, so you may see a separate block for each currency you use.

## How do I run it?

1. Click **Reports** in the sidebar.
2. Choose **Revenue**.
3. Pick a range from the date selector: **This month**, **Last month**, **Quarter**, **Year** or **Custom**.
4. Optionally filter by client using the **Client** drop-down.
5. Read the totals, or click **Export CSV** (Pro and Business) to download the table.

Date rules, including how time zones affect which day an invoice falls on, are covered in Date ranges and time zones.

## How is Revenue different from Payments received?

The Revenue report is based on invoice dates and shows what you billed. The Payments received report is based on the date money actually arrived and lists every payment. Use Revenue to see how busy you have been, and use Payments received to check your bank deposits.

| Question | Report |
| --- | --- |
| How much did I bill last quarter? | Revenue |
| Who still owes me money, and for how long? | Aging |
| Which payments landed this week? | Payments received |

## Why don't my numbers match my bank?

Common reasons are payments still pending, such as ACH payments that take up to 2 business days, processing fees deducted from payouts, and payouts that arrive up to 2 business days after the payment. Offline payments such as cheques also appear only after you record them with **Record payment**.
