# Understanding the dashboard

The Dashboard is the first page you see after signing in. It gives you a quick view of money owed, money collected and invoices that need attention. Open it any time from **Dashboard** in the left sidebar.

## What are the summary cards?

Four cards sit across the top:

- **Outstanding**: the total unpaid balance on invoices with status Sent or Overdue.
- **Overdue**: the part of that balance that is past its due date, with the number of invoices.
- **Paid this month**: payments received since the 1st of the current month.
- **Draft**: the number and value of invoices you have not sent yet.

Cards show amounts in your account currency. If you bill clients in other currencies, click a card to see the breakdown by currency.

## What does the chart show?

Below the cards is a bar chart of the last 6 months. Each month has two bars: invoiced and collected. Hover over a bar to see the exact figure. The chart uses your account time zone, so the month boundaries match your reports. For deeper analysis, open the Revenue report.

## What is in the lists?

Two lists sit under the chart:

1. **Needs attention**: Overdue invoices and invoices that will be due within 7 days, with the client name and amount. Click a row to open the invoice and send a reminder or record a payment.
2. **Recent activity**: the latest events, such as an invoice sent, a payment received, or an email bounce. Click **View all** to open the full Activity log.

Disputed invoices show a **Disputed** tag in the attention list.

## Can I change the dashboard?

The layout is fixed, but you can change the period. Use the selector at the top right to switch the cards between **This month**, **Last month**, **Quarter** and **Year**. The Outstanding and Overdue cards always show the balance as of today, whatever period is selected.

## Why don't the dashboard numbers match a report?

The dashboard is a summary, so small differences can appear:

- Dashboard cards include Sent and Overdue invoices only, while the Revenue report also counts Paid ones.
- Pending ACH payments are not counted in Paid this month until they clear, which can take up to 2 business days.
- Offline payments appear only after you record them with **Record payment**.

| Card | Based on |
| --- | --- |
| Outstanding | Unpaid balance, as of today |
| Overdue | Unpaid balance past due date |
| Paid this month | Payment date |
| Draft | Invoices not yet sent |

For a client-by-client view of overdue amounts, use the Aging report.
