# Choosing payment terms

Payment terms decide how many days a client has to pay before an invoice becomes overdue. Ledgerly defaults to Net 30, and you can change the terms for your whole account, for a single client, or for one invoice.

## What payment term options are available?

| Option | Due date |
|--------|----------|
| Due on receipt | The day the invoice is issued |
| Net 7 | 7 days after the invoice date |
| Net 14 | 14 days after the invoice date |
| Net 15 | 15 days after the invoice date |
| Net 30 | 30 days after the invoice date (default) |
| Net 45 | 45 days after the invoice date |
| Net 60 | 60 days after the invoice date |
| Custom date | A specific date you pick |

The due date is calculated from the invoice date, not from the day you send the invoice. If you create an invoice dated the 1st and send it on the 5th, Net 30 still makes it due on the 31st.

## How do I change my default payment terms?

1. Go to **Settings → Invoices**.
2. Find **Default payment terms**.
3. Pick a new option from the list.
4. Save.

The new default applies to invoices you create from now on. Existing invoices keep their original terms.

## How do I set payment terms for one client?

Open **Clients**, select the client, choose Edit, and change **Default payment terms**. Every new invoice for that client starts with those terms instead of the account default. This is useful when one large customer insists on Net 60 while everyone else pays on Net 14.

You can still override the terms on any individual invoice while you are creating it. Just change the **Payment terms** field near the invoice date.

## How do payment terms affect overdue status, reminders, and late fees?

Everything is measured from the due date.

- The invoice becomes Overdue the day after the due date, set automatically around 1 a.m. in your account's time zone.
- Automatic reminders go out at 3, 7 and 14 days past due, and optionally 3 days before the due date.
- A late fee, if you have them turned on, starts 30 days after the due date.

Shorter terms therefore mean you see overdue invoices sooner. Choosing Due on receipt means the invoice turns Overdue the following day. See Understanding late fees and Payment reminders for the details of each.

## Which terms should I pick?

Common patterns:

- Net 7 or Net 14 for small one-off jobs.
- Net 30 for retainers and most business clients.
- Net 45 or Net 60 only when a client's accounts-payable process requires it.
- Custom date for milestones with a fixed deadline.

The terms appear on the invoice and the payment page.
