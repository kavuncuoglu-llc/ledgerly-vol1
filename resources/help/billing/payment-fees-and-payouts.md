# Payment fees and payouts

Ledgerly charges a small fee on each successful online payment and has no monthly fees on top of your plan. Money is paid out to your connected bank account within 2 business days.

## What does it cost to accept online payments?

| Payment method | Fee |
|----------------|-----|
| Card | 2.9% + 30¢ per successful charge |
| Bank transfer (ACH, US only) | 0.8%, capped at $5 |

For example, a $1,000.00 card payment costs $29.30 in fees ($29.00 plus 30¢), so you receive $970.70. A $1,000.00 ACH payment would cost 0.8%, which is $8.00, but the $5 cap applies, so you pay $5.00 and receive $995.00.

Fees apply only when a payment succeeds. Failed or declined payments cost nothing. Online payments are available on the Pro and Business plans.

## When do I get paid?

Payouts go to your connected bank account within 2 business days of a successful payment. Your first payout can take up to 7 days while the account is verified. After that, the 2-business-day timing applies.

ACH payments can show as pending for up to 2 business days before they are confirmed. The invoice updates once the payment clears.

## How do I connect my bank account?

1. Go to **Settings → Payments**.
2. Choose **Connect bank account**.
3. Follow the steps to verify your business details and bank account.
4. Save.

Payouts are supported for businesses in the US, Canada, the UK, Australia, and most of the EU. Card numbers are never stored by Ledgerly.

## How do I see what I have been paid?

Open **Payments** in the left sidebar to see every payment, its status, the fee, and the payout it belongs to. The Payments received report under **Reports** summarizes totals for a date range such as this month, last month, quarter, year, or custom. Pro and Business plans can export it to CSV.

## What about refunds and chargebacks?

When you refund a payment, the processing fee is not returned, and the refunded amount is deducted from a future payout. Chargebacks carry a $15 dispute fee and are managed under **Dashboard → Payments → Disputes**. See Refunding a payment and Disputing a charge.

## How do I turn a payment method off?

Under **Settings → Payments** you can toggle card or bank transfer so clients only see the methods you want. If you prefer to avoid card fees, turn card off and leave ACH on, but be aware ACH is for US clients only. Payments made outside Ledgerly, such as a check, have no Ledgerly fee; record them with **Record payment**.
