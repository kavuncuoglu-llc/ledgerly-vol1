# Deposits and partial payments

Ledgerly supports payments that cover only part of an invoice. A deposit or partial payment reduces the outstanding balance, and the invoice stays open until the full amount is received.

## How do partial payments work?

When a payment is smaller than the invoice total, the invoice does not become Paid. It stays Sent (or Overdue if it is already past due) and the outstanding balance goes down by the amount received. The invoice only becomes Paid when the full amount has been received.

For example, if a $2,000.00 invoice receives a $500.00 payment, the invoice shows $1,500.00 outstanding. The client sees the updated balance on the payment page and can pay any amount up to what remains.

## How do I record a deposit or partial payment?

If the client paid outside Ledgerly (check, cash, or a bank transfer to your own account), record it manually:

1. Open the invoice.
2. Choose **Record payment**.
3. Enter the amount, the date, and the method: card, bank transfer, check, cash, or other.
4. Save.

If you enter less than the balance, the invoice stays open. If you enter the full balance, it becomes Paid. Payments made on the payment page are recorded automatically and update the invoice within a minute. Bank transfers (ACH) may show as pending for up to 2 business days.

## How do I ask for a deposit up front?

Ledgerly does not have a separate deposit field. A common approach is to create two invoices for the same job: a deposit invoice, such as 50% of the estimate, and a final invoice for the balance. Name the line items clearly, for example "Website design, 50% deposit", so the client understands each one. When the final invoice is created, add a line describing the deposit that was already paid.

Alternatively, create a single invoice and record the deposit with **Record payment** once it arrives.

## How do partial payments affect reminders and late fees?

A partial payment does not stop reminders, because the invoice is still unpaid. Late fees are calculated on the remaining balance, excluding any previous late fees, so a partial payment lowers the fee going forward. See Understanding late fees for a worked example.

If a client cannot pay the remainder at once, a payment plan may be easier to manage than several ad hoc payments. See Setting up a payment plan.

## How do I correct a partial payment recorded by mistake?

Open the invoice, find the payment in the payment history, and delete or edit it. The outstanding balance and status update immediately. If the money was taken online by card and needs to go back to the client, use a refund instead; see Refunding a payment.

Each payment, whether partial or full, triggers a receipt email to the client automatically.
