# Managing billing contacts for a client

Each client in Ledgerly can have up to 3 billing contacts. Invoices, reminders, receipts and statements are emailed to these addresses, so keeping them accurate is the simplest way to get paid on time.

## What is a billing contact?

A billing contact is an email address on a client record that receives billing emails. You might add the person who hired you, their finance team, and a shared accounts-payable mailbox. All three receive the same message at the same time.

Clients are called customers in the app, but the two words mean the same thing.

## How do I add or change billing contacts?

1. Open **Clients** and select the client.
2. Choose Edit.
3. In the email section, add up to 3 addresses.
4. Save.

The change applies immediately to emails sent from then on. Emails already sent are not re-sent. If you try to add a fourth address, Ledgerly asks you to remove one first.

## Which emails go to billing contacts?

All of these go to every contact on the client record:

- Invoice emails, which include the PDF and the secure payment link.
- Automatic and manual payment reminders.
- Payment receipts.
- Statements of account.
- Payment plan schedules.

If you send an invoice by email, you can change the recipients for that one send without editing the client record. See Sending an invoice by email.

## What if an email is not delivered?

A "not delivered" entry in an invoice's Activity log usually means the client's mailbox rejected the message. Common causes are a typo in the address, a full inbox, or a strict spam filter. To fix it:

1. Confirm the address with the client.
2. Correct it on the client record.
3. Resend the invoice or send a manual reminder (one per invoice every 24 hours).

On the Business plan you can send from your own domain, which often improves delivery. Otherwise, emails come from `invoices@mail.ledgerly.example`, and asking the client to allow that address helps.

## Can clients see their invoices without email?

Yes. Clients can use the client portal, a secure link showing all their invoices and letting them pay. It needs no password. They enter their email and receive a magic-link email. Only addresses listed as billing contacts can sign in, so updating contacts also controls who has access.

Archiving a client hides them but keeps their history; billing contacts on an archived client do not receive new automatic emails. A client can only be deleted if they have no invoices.
