# What clients see on the payment page

When you email an invoice, it includes a secure payment link. The link opens a Ledgerly payment page where the client can review the invoice and pay online. No account or password is required.

## What does the payment page show?

The page shows the invoice as the client would see it in the PDF, with these key details at the top:

- Your business name, logo and accent color (custom logo and colors need Pro or Business).
- The invoice number, such as INV-4471, the invoice date and the due date.
- Line items with description, quantity, unit price and tax.
- Discounts, taxes and the total.
- Any late fee, shown as a separate line and included in the total.
- Payments already received and the outstanding balance.
- Your invoice footer text and, if you added them, attached files.

The amount to pay is always the current outstanding balance.

## How can clients pay?

Clients can pay by card or by bank transfer (ACH, US only), depending on which methods you have switched on. Card payments cost you 2.9% + 30¢ per successful charge. ACH costs 0.8%, capped at $5. Clients are never charged extra by Ledgerly.

After paying, the client receives a receipt email automatically. The invoice updates within a minute for card payments. ACH can show as pending for up to 2 business days. Card numbers are never stored by Ledgerly.

## How do I choose which payment methods appear?

1. Go to **Settings → Payments**.
2. Toggle **Card** and **Bank transfer (ACH)** on or off.
3. Save.

At least one method must stay on for online payments to work. Online payments need the Pro or Business plan and a connected bank account for payouts. See Payment fees and payouts.

## Can clients pay part of the balance?

Yes. A client can enter a smaller amount, and the invoice stays open with a reduced balance. See Deposits and partial payments. If a payment plan is active, the page lists each installment and its due date instead.

## What if the invoice is already paid or voided?

A paid invoice shows a Paid label and a link to the receipt, with no pay button. A voided invoice shows a Void label and cannot be paid. The link stays valid so the client can still view the record.

The payment page also links to the client portal, where the client can see all of their invoices from you. To see exactly what a client sees, open any invoice and choose **Preview payment page**. The preview does not charge anything and does not mark the invoice as viewed.
