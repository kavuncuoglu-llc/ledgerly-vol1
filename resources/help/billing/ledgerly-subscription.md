# Billing for your Ledgerly subscription

This article covers what you pay Ledgerly for your plan, how to upgrade or downgrade, and where to find your own Ledgerly invoices. It is separate from the invoices you send to your clients.

## What do the plans cost?

| Plan | Monthly | Annual |
|------|---------|--------|
| Free | $0 | $0 |
| Pro | $19 | $190 |
| Business | $49 | $490 |

Annual billing gives you 2 months free compared with paying monthly. Pro includes unlimited clients and invoices, recurring invoices, late fees, automatic reminders, online payments, custom logo and colors, CSV export, and 1 user. Business adds up to 5 team seats, roles, API access, accounting integrations, a custom sending domain, and priority support. Free allows up to 3 clients and 5 invoices per calendar month.

New accounts get a 14-day Pro trial with no card needed.

## How do I upgrade my plan?

1. Go to **Settings → Account → Subscription**.
2. Choose the plan and whether to pay monthly or annually.
3. Enter your card details.
4. Confirm.

Upgrades take effect immediately, so features such as late fees and automatic reminders unlock right away.

## How do I downgrade?

Choose a lower plan on the same page. Downgrading takes effect at the end of your current billing period, so you keep your current features until then. Your data is never deleted when you downgrade. Features lock instead. For example, after moving to Free you keep all your clients and invoices, but recurring invoices, late fees and automatic reminders stop working until you upgrade again. Free also limits you to 5 new invoices per calendar month.

## Where do I find invoices from Ledgerly?

Ledgerly emails a receipt to the account owner after each payment for your plan. You can also view and download them under **Settings → Account → Subscription**. To change the card used, update it on the same page.

## What happens when the trial ends?

When the 14-day Pro trial ends without a card on file, the account moves to the Free plan. Nothing is deleted. Add a card and choose Pro or Business at any time to turn Pro features back on.

## How do I get help with billing questions?

Pro and Business accounts can use the in-app chat widget, with a reply within 1 business day, or within 4 business hours on Business. Any plan can email support@ledgerly.example, and Free plan replies take up to 2 business days. If you close your account under **Settings → Account → Close account**, your data is kept for 30 days for recovery and then deleted.
