# Refunding a payment

You can refund a card or bank payment received through Ledgerly, either in full or in part, within 90 days of the payment. This article explains how, and what happens to fees and the invoice.

## What are the refund rules?

- Refunds can be full or partial.
- A payment can be refunded within 90 days of the date it was received.
- Processing fees are not returned. If a client paid $100.00 by card, you refund up to $100.00, but the 2.9% + 30¢ fee on the original charge is not given back.
- Refunds only apply to payments taken online through Ledgerly. If you recorded a payment manually (check, cash, other), there is nothing to send back through Ledgerly. Delete or edit the recorded payment and repay the client yourself.

## How do I issue a refund?

1. Go to **Payments** in the left sidebar.
2. Find the payment, or open the invoice and look at its payment history.
3. Choose **Refund**.
4. Enter the amount. It cannot exceed the amount received.
5. Add an optional note for your records.
6. Confirm.

The client receives an email confirming the refund. Card refunds usually reach the client's statement in a few business days, depending on their bank.

## What happens to the invoice after a refund?

The invoice balance goes back up by the refunded amount. Using a $500.00 invoice paid in full as an example:

| Action | Invoice status | Outstanding balance |
|--------|----------------|---------------------|
| Paid in full | Paid | $0.00 |
| $500.00 refunded | Sent or Overdue | $500.00 |
| $200.00 refunded | Sent or Overdue | $200.00 |

If the invoice is now unpaid and past its due date, it returns to Overdue and normal reminders and late fee rules apply. To avoid that, mark the invoice as disputed, or void it if the sale is cancelled. See Voiding an invoice.

## Refund or chargeback: which is better?

If a client is unhappy, a refund is usually cheaper and faster than a chargeback. A chargeback happens when the client asks their card issuer to reverse the payment, and it carries a $15 dispute fee, handled under **Dashboard → Payments → Disputes**. Issuing a refund promptly when it is deserved often avoids that. See Disputing a charge for the difference between invoice disputes and chargebacks.

## Where can I see refund history?

Refunds appear in the payment history of the invoice and under **Payments**. The Payments received report shows net amounts after refunds. You can export it to CSV on Pro and Business plans.

Refunds are only possible for online payments, which are available on Pro and Business. Your payout is adjusted automatically, so the refunded amount is taken from your next payout.
