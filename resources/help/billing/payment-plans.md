# Setting up a payment plan

A payment plan lets a client pay an overdue or large invoice in installments. While the plan is on track, Ledgerly pauses late fees and reminders for that invoice.

## What is a payment plan?

A payment plan splits the outstanding balance of one invoice into 2 to 12 installments. You choose the schedule, and each installment is a fixed share of the balance. The invoice itself does not change: it stays Sent or Overdue until the final installment is paid, and then it becomes Paid.

Payment plans are meant for clients who are willing to pay but need more time. They are separate from partial payments, which are one-off amounts you record against an invoice.

## How do I set up a payment plan?

1. Open **Invoices** and select the unpaid invoice.
2. Choose **⋯ → Set up payment plan**.
3. Choose the number of installments, from 2 to 12.
4. Choose the frequency: weekly, every 2 weeks, or monthly.
5. Pick the date of the first installment.
6. Review the schedule and confirm.

The client is emailed the schedule. Each installment appears on the payment page so they can pay it by card or bank transfer (ACH, US only). Online payments update the invoice within a minute.

## What happens to late fees and reminders during a plan?

While the plan is on track, late fees and automatic reminders are paused. If a late fee had already been applied before you created the plan, it stays on the invoice and is included in the balance being split. Waive it first if you do not want to collect it; see Understanding late fees.

Installments paid outside Ledgerly, for example by check, must be recorded with **Record payment** so the plan stays on track.

## What happens if an installment is missed?

If an installment is missed by more than 7 days, the plan is cancelled automatically. Normal rules then resume:

- Reminders restart on the usual schedule.
- Late fees resume under the usual rules. Amounts already paid reduce the outstanding balance.
- You can create a new plan on the same invoice if you agree a new arrangement.

An installment that is late by 7 days or fewer does not cancel the plan, so a short delay is forgiven.

## Can I cancel or change a plan?

You can cancel a plan at any time from the invoice by choosing **⋯ → Cancel payment plan**. Cancelling returns the invoice to normal rules straight away. To change the number of installments, cancel the plan and create a new one. Payments already received stay recorded and reduce the balance of the new plan.

Payment plans are available on Pro and Business plans.
