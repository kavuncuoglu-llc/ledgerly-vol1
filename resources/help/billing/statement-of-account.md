# Sending a statement of account

A statement of account lists every invoice and payment for one client over a period, along with the balance owed. It is a quick way to show a client everything outstanding in a single document.

## What does a client statement contain?

A statement shows, for the date range you choose:

- Each invoice issued, with its number, date, due date and total.
- Each payment received, with its date and method.
- Credits and late fees, shown on their own lines.
- The opening balance, the closing balance, and the total currently overdue.

The statement uses the client's currency and is branded with your logo and colors. Free plan statements carry Ledgerly branding, like invoices.

## How do I create a statement?

1. Go to **Reports → Client statements**.
2. Choose the client.
3. Pick a date range: this month, last month, quarter, year, or custom.
4. Select **Generate**.

The statement appears on screen. You can then download it as a PDF or email it to the client. PDF statements are available on all plans.

## How do I email a statement to a client?

From the generated statement, choose **Send**. The email goes to all billing contacts on the client record (up to 3 addresses). It includes the PDF and a link to the client portal, where the client can see every invoice and pay what is open. The portal needs no password; clients sign in with a magic link sent to their email.

You can edit the message before sending. Each send is recorded in the client's activity history.

## When should I send a statement?

Statements are useful when a client has several invoices and may have lost track of them. Typical moments:

- At the end of each month for clients with multiple open invoices.
- Before a follow-up call about overdue payments.
- When the client's accountant asks for a full history.

A statement is a summary, not a payment demand, so it does not pause or start reminders and does not trigger late fees. See Payment reminders for the automatic schedule.

## How is a statement different from the Aging report?

The Aging report covers your whole account and groups unpaid invoices by how late they are: current, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90, and 90+ days. A client statement covers one client and includes paid items too. Use Aging to decide who to chase, and a statement to show one client what they owe.

Reports can be exported as CSV on Pro and Business plans. Statements always export as PDF, and everything can be downloaded at once under **Settings → Account → Export my data**.
