# Auto-send vs draft for recurring invoices

Every recurring run creates an invoice. The Auto-send setting decides whether that invoice is emailed to your client immediately or saved as a Draft for you to review first.

## What is the difference between auto-send and draft?

| | Auto-send on | Auto-send off (draft) |
|---|---|---|
| Invoice status after the run | Sent | Draft |
| Emailed to client | Yes, immediately | No, you send it |
| Payment link included | Yes | Added when you send |
| Reminders and late fees start | From the due date as normal | Only after you send it |

With Auto-send on, the email contains the PDF and a secure payment link, exactly like an invoice you send by hand. It goes to every billing contact on the client record (up to 3).

## How do I choose the setting?

When creating or editing a schedule in **Recurring**, find the **Delivery** option and pick **Auto-send** or **Create as draft**. You can switch it at any time. A change applies to the next run onward.

## When should I use auto-send?

Use Auto-send when the invoice is the same every time, so there is nothing to check:

- Fixed monthly retainers
- Flat-rate maintenance or hosting
- Memberships and subscriptions
- Rent

It saves you a step and means the client gets the invoice on the same day each cycle, which tends to make payment more predictable.

## When should I use drafts?

Use drafts when something might need adjusting before the client sees it:

- Hours or quantities change each month
- You add one-off expenses
- A discount applies some months
- You want to attach a timesheet (up to 5 files, 10 MB each)

On the day of the run, the draft appears in your Invoices list with a Draft status and you receive a notification. Open it, edit what you need, and click **Send**. Until you send it, the client sees nothing and no reminders go out.

## What if I forget to send a draft?

Drafts do not expire and nothing is sent automatically. A draft left alone never becomes Overdue, because it was never delivered. To help avoid forgetting, Ledgerly emails the account owner a daily summary while a recurring draft is still unsent 3 days after its run.

## Which client details does auto-send need?

Auto-send requires at least one billing contact email on the client. If the client has none, the invoice is still created, but it is left as a Draft and the run is flagged. See What happens when a recurring run fails. Archived clients are not sent invoices either.
