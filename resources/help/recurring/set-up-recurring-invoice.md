# Set up a recurring invoice

Yes, you can make an invoice repeat every month. Create a recurring schedule once, and Ledgerly generates a fresh invoice on every run, so you never rebuild the same invoice by hand.

## Who can use recurring invoices?

Recurring invoices are available on the Pro and Business plans, including the 14-day Pro trial. The Free plan does not include them. If you downgrade, your schedules and their history are kept, but they stop generating invoices until you upgrade again.

Recurring invoices suit any work that repeats on a predictable rhythm: monthly retainer clients, maintenance contracts, rent, memberships, hosting, or a weekly service.

## How do I create a recurring schedule?

1. In the left sidebar, open **Recurring** and click **New recurring invoice**.
2. Choose the client. The client's currency and default payment terms are filled in for you.
3. Add line items. Each line has a description, quantity, unit price and an optional tax rate. You can add up to 50 line items.
4. Pick an **Interval** (see the table below).
5. Set the **Start date**. Optionally set an **End date** or a **Number of occurrences**.
6. Choose **Auto-send** or **Create as draft** (see below).
7. Click **Save schedule**.

The schedule appears in the Recurring list with its next run date.

## What intervals are available?

| Interval | Creates an invoice |
|---|---|
| Weekly | Every 7 days |
| Every 2 weeks | Every 14 days |
| Monthly | Once a month, on the same day |
| Quarterly | Every 3 months |
| Yearly | Once a year |

If a monthly schedule starts on the 31st, shorter months use their last day instead.

## Should I choose auto-send or draft?

Each run creates a new invoice. With **Auto-send** on, Ledgerly emails the invoice to the client's billing contacts straight away, with the PDF and a secure payment link. With it off, the invoice is saved as a Draft so you can review it, adjust it, and send it yourself.

A good rule: use Auto-send for fixed-price retainers that never change, and use drafts when each invoice needs a check, such as billable hours that vary. The article Auto-send vs draft for recurring invoices goes into more detail.

## How do I set up a retainer client?

For a fixed monthly retainer, create a schedule with the Monthly interval, one line item describing the retainer, no end date, and Auto-send turned on. Set the client's payment terms (for example Net 15) on the client record, and the due date of every generated invoice follows from it. Leave the end date empty so the retainer runs until you end it.

## When does the first invoice go out?

If the start date is today, the first invoice is generated within a few minutes of saving. For a future start date, the first run happens around 6 a.m. in your account's time zone on that day. Generated invoices take the next number in your normal sequence, such as INV-4471.
