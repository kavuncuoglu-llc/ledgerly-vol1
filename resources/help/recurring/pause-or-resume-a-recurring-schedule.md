# Pause or resume a recurring schedule

You can pause a recurring schedule at any time to stop it generating invoices, and resume it later without recreating anything. Pausing does not delete the schedule or the invoices it has already created.

## How do I pause a schedule?

1. Open **Recurring** in the left sidebar.
2. Find the schedule in the list. Each row shows the client, interval, next run date and status.
3. Click **⋯ → Pause**.
4. Confirm. The status changes from Active to Paused, and the next run date is cleared.

You can also open the schedule and click **Pause schedule** at the top of the page. A paused schedule creates no invoices and sends no emails.

## When should I pause instead of ending a schedule?

Pause when you expect to bill the client again, for example:

- A client is on holiday or between projects for a few months.
- You are renegotiating the price and do not want a new invoice at the old rate.
- A client has an unpaid balance and you want to hold further billing while you talk.
- A contract is on hold pending renewal.

If the work has finished for good, end the schedule instead. The article Edit or end a recurring schedule explains how.

## What happens to invoices that already exist?

Nothing. Invoices generated before the pause keep their status. Sent invoices still get reminders, still accrue late fees under the usual rules, and can still be paid through their payment links. Drafts already created stay as drafts until you send or delete them.

## What happens when I resume?

1. Open **Recurring**, then click **⋯ → Resume** on the paused schedule.
2. Ledgerly asks for the next run date. The default is the next date on the schedule's original rhythm that falls in the future.
3. Click **Resume schedule**.

Ledgerly does not create catch-up invoices for the runs that were skipped while paused. If you paused a monthly schedule for three months, you get one new invoice on the next run date, not three. If you do want to bill for the missed months, create a regular invoice for them, or choose a next run date of today so the schedule runs immediately.

If the schedule has an end date or a number of occurrences, skipped runs do not count toward the total. A schedule set to 12 occurrences that was paused after 4 still has 8 left.

## Does a paused schedule count against any limit?

No. Paused schedules do not count toward any usage limit, and Pro and Business have no cap on the number of schedules. Schedules also pause automatically if the account falls back to the Free plan after a downgrade takes effect. When you upgrade again, resume them from the Recurring list.
