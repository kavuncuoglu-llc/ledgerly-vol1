# Creating an invoice

You can create an invoice in about a minute: choose a client, add line items, and save it as a Draft or send it right away.

## How do I create a new invoice?

1. Click **Invoices** in the left sidebar.
2. Choose **New invoice**.
3. Select a client, or choose **Add new client** to create one on the spot.
4. Review the invoice date and payment terms.
5. Add line items.
6. Choose **Save as draft** or **Save and send**.

The invoice gets the next number automatically, such as INV-4471. The prefix and next number can be changed under **Settings → Invoices**; see Invoice numbering.

## What information does an invoice need?

| Field | Notes |
|-------|-------|
| Client | Sets the currency and default payment terms |
| Invoice date | Defaults to today |
| Payment terms | Default is Net 30; the due date is calculated from the invoice date |
| Line items | Description, quantity, unit price, optional tax rate; up to 50 per invoice |
| Discount | Optional percent or fixed amount for the whole invoice |
| Notes and footer | Optional message and footer text such as bank details |

The total must be at least your minimum invoice amount, $1.00 by default. See Setting minimum invoice amounts.

## How do payment terms and due dates work?

Each new invoice picks up the payment terms from the client, or from your account default if the client has none. You can change them for this invoice only, from Due on receipt up to Net 60, or choose a custom date. The due date is shown next to the terms and updates as you change them. See Choosing payment terms.

## Should I save as a draft or send immediately?

A Draft is private. You can edit it, preview the PDF, and send it later. Reminders and late fees only begin once the invoice has been sent and a due date passes.

Choose **Save and send** when you are happy with the invoice. Ledgerly emails the client a message with the PDF and a secure payment link, and the status changes to Sent. See Sending an invoice by email.

## Are there limits on the Free plan?

Yes. Free accounts can have up to 3 clients and 5 invoices per calendar month, and PDFs carry Ledgerly branding. Pro and Business plans have unlimited clients and invoices.

## How can I create invoices faster?

Use these shortcuts:

- Duplicate an existing invoice from **⋯ → Duplicate** when you bill the same client for similar work. The copy is a new Draft with a new number.
- Set up a recurring schedule under **Recurring** for work you bill every week, month, or year.
- Save a default invoice footer and email message so you do not retype them.

Pick a template, Classic, Modern or Compact, to change how the PDF looks without changing the content.
