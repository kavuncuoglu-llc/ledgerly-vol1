# Attaching files to an invoice

You can attach supporting documents to an invoice, such as a signed estimate, a timesheet, or receipts for expenses. Your client can view or download them from the payment page.

## What are the attachment limits?

| Limit | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Files per invoice | Up to 5 |
| Size per file | Up to 10 MB |

Attachments are available on every plan. Files larger than 10 MB, or a sixth file, will be rejected with a message. Compress large files, or share them through a link in the invoice notes instead.

## How do I attach a file?

1. Open the invoice in edit mode, or start a new one.
2. Scroll to **Attachments**.
3. Choose **Add file**, or drag a file onto the area.
4. Wait for the upload to finish.
5. Save the invoice.

To remove a file, click the X next to its name and save. You can attach files to a Draft at any time. Once an invoice has been sent, changes to attachments are saved but are not emailed again unless you resend the invoice.

## What does the client see?

Attachments are listed on the payment page, where the client can open or download each file. When you send the invoice by email, the client receives the invoice PDF and the payment link. Attached files are available through the link, so the email stays small.

The invoice PDF itself does not embed your attachments. If you download the PDF from Ledgerly, the attachments are downloaded separately. See Downloading an invoice as a PDF.

## Why is my PDF or email failing with attachments?

PDFs fail on very large attachments. If a download or email fails and the invoice has several big files:

1. Remove the largest attachment and try again.
2. Compress images or scanned documents before uploading.
3. Reduce the number of files by combining documents into one PDF.

Keeping total attachments small also makes the payment page load faster for your client. If the problem remains, email support@ledgerly.example with the invoice number.

## Which files should I attach?

Good candidates include:

- Signed estimates or contracts that justify the amounts
- Timesheets or work logs
- Expense receipts you are passing on to the client
- Delivery confirmations

Avoid attaching anything confidential that the client does not need, since anyone with the secure payment link can view the files. If a client has several billing contacts, each of them receives the same link.

Attachments are copied when you duplicate an invoice, so remove any that no longer apply. They are retained on voided invoices for your records. See Voiding an invoice.
