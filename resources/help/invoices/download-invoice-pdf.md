# Downloading an invoice as a PDF

You can download any invoice as a PDF to print it, attach it to your own email, or keep a copy. The PDF matches what your client receives when you send the invoice from Ledgerly.

## How do I download a single invoice?

1. Click **Invoices** in the left sidebar.
2. Open the invoice.
3. Choose **Download PDF**.

The file is named after the invoice number, for example INV-4471.pdf. Downloading works for invoices in any status, including Draft, Paid, and Void. A Draft PDF is useful for previewing the layout before you send it.

## What appears on the PDF?

The PDF shows your logo and business details, the client's billing details and tax ID if you have saved them, the invoice number and dates, all line items, discounts, taxes, any late fee as a separate line, and the total. Your footer text appears at the bottom, and Paid invoices show a Paid label.

Voided invoices show a Void mark across the document so nobody mistakes them for a live bill. A custom logo and colors need the Pro or Business plan. On the Free plan, PDFs carry Ledgerly branding.

## How do I change how the PDF looks?

Ledgerly offers three templates: Classic, Modern, and Compact. Choose one under **Settings → Invoices** or from the invoice's template menu. Templates change the layout and typography only, not the content. Compact is helpful for invoices with many line items.

Your accent color, logo (PNG, JPG, or SVG up to 2 MB), and footer text apply to all templates.

## Why won't my PDF download?

If the download fails, try these steps:

- Check your attachments. PDFs fail on very large attachments, so remove or compress big files. You can attach up to 5 files of 10 MB each, but smaller files are more reliable.
- Check your browser. PDF export works in Safari 16 and later. In older versions, update Safari or use another browser.
- Reduce the line items if the invoice is very long. The limit is 50 line items per invoice.
- Try again in a private window to rule out an extension blocking the download.

If it still fails, email support@ledgerly.example with the invoice number.

## Can I download many invoices at once?

You can export your invoice list as a CSV on Pro and Business plans from the **Reports** area. To get all your invoices as PDFs at once, use **Settings → Account → Export my data**, which produces a ZIP of CSV files and PDFs. PDF statements for a single client are available on all plans under **Reports → Client statements**.
