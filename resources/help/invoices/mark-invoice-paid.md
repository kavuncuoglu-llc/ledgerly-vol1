# How to mark an invoice as paid

When a client pays you outside Ledgerly, record the payment so the invoice shows as Paid. You can do it with **Record payment**, which captures the details, or with the **Mark as paid** shortcut.

## When do I need to mark an invoice as paid manually?

Online payments update the invoice automatically within a minute. You only need to record a payment yourself when the money did not go through Ledgerly. Examples include a check, cash, or a bank transfer sent straight to your own account.

This is the usual reason behind "Payment received but invoice still shows unpaid". Ledgerly cannot see money that arrived elsewhere, so the invoice stays Sent or Overdue, and reminders and late fees continue until you record the payment.

## How do I record a payment?

1. Open **Invoices** and select the invoice.
2. Choose **Record payment**.
3. Enter the amount received. It defaults to the outstanding balance.
4. Choose the date the money arrived.
5. Choose the method: card, bank transfer, check, cash, or other.
6. Save.

If the amount equals the balance, the invoice becomes Paid. If it is smaller, the invoice stays open with a lower balance; see Deposits and partial payments. Ledgerly emails a receipt to the client for every recorded payment.

## What does the Mark as paid shortcut do?

Mark as paid records a payment for the full outstanding balance in one click, using today's date. Use it when the client has paid in full and you do not need to note the method or a different date.

Choose **Record payment** instead if the date is in the past or you want the method on file. A correct date matters for the Payments received report and for working out how long clients take to pay.

## What happens after an invoice is marked as paid?

- The status changes to Paid.
- Automatic reminders stop.
- Late fees stop accruing. Any fee already charged stays on the invoice.
- The payment appears in the invoice's payment history and in the Payments received report.
- The payment page shows a Paid label with the receipt.

## How do I fix a payment I recorded by mistake?

Open the invoice, find the payment in the payment history, and edit or delete it. The status and balance update immediately. If you deleted the only payment on an overdue invoice, the invoice returns to Overdue.

If money was taken online through Ledgerly and needs to go back to the client, use Refunding a payment instead. Recorded manual payments cannot be refunded through Ledgerly.
