# Adding line items and quantities

Line items are the individual products or services listed on an invoice. Each one has a description, a quantity, a unit price, and an optional tax rate. Ledgerly multiplies quantity by unit price and adds the lines together.

## What does a line item contain?

| Field | Purpose |
|-------|---------|
| Description | What you are charging for |
| Quantity | How many units, hours or days |
| Unit price | Price of one unit in the client's currency |
| Tax rate | Optional, chosen from the rates in Settings → Taxes |

The line amount is quantity multiplied by unit price. For example, 12.5 hours at $80.00 is $1,000.00. Quantities can include decimals, which suits hourly billing.

## How do I add line items?

1. Open an invoice in edit mode or start a new one.
2. Choose **Add line item**.
3. Fill in the description, quantity and unit price.
4. Pick a tax rate if one applies.
5. Repeat for more lines, then save.

You can drag lines to reorder them and use the delete icon to remove one. Totals update as you type.

## How many line items can an invoice have?

An invoice can have up to 50 line items. If you reach the limit, combine similar items, for example by grouping hours by week, or split the work across two invoices. Large invoices with many lines and big attachments are also more likely to have PDF problems, so shorter is safer.

## How do taxes work on line items?

Each line can carry up to 5 tax rates. Rates are defined in **Settings → Taxes** with a name, a rate, and whether they are inclusive or exclusive. Exclusive tax is added on top of the line amount. Inclusive tax is already part of the price you enter.

For example, a $100.00 line with a 10% exclusive tax shows $10.00 of tax and a total of $110.00. The Tax summary report shows tax collected per rate and period. Ledgerly does not file taxes or give tax advice.

## What is a good way to write descriptions?

Clear descriptions help clients approve invoices faster. Include the project name, the dates or period, and what was delivered, for instance "Brand refresh, design hours, 3 to 14 June". Avoid vague text such as "Services".

Descriptions can be used for non-billable notes too, such as a line with quantity 1 and a price of $0.00 that says "Deposit already paid, see INV-4402". Remember that a total below the minimum invoice amount cannot be saved; see Setting minimum invoice amounts.

You can also add a discount for the whole invoice; see Adding discounts to an invoice.
