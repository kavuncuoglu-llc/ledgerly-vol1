# Setting minimum invoice amounts

The minimum invoice amount is an account setting that stops you from saving an invoice whose total is below a set value. The default is $1.00.

## What is the minimum invoice amount?

It is a safeguard against accidental tiny or empty invoices, such as one saved before the line items were filled in. If an invoice total falls below the minimum, Ledgerly will not let you save it and shows a message explaining why.

The minimum applies to the invoice total after discounts and taxes. An invoice for $0.50 with the default minimum of $1.00 cannot be saved. A $1.00 invoice can.

## How do I change the minimum invoice amount?

1. Go to **Settings → Invoices**.
2. Find **Minimum invoice amount**.
3. Enter the new amount, for example $25.00.
4. Save.

The new minimum applies to invoices you save from now on. Invoices that already exist are not affected, even if their total is lower than the new minimum, but you may be asked to raise the total if you edit and re-save a Draft that is below it.

## Why can't I save my invoice?

If you see a message about the minimum amount, check these causes:

- A line item has a quantity or unit price of zero.
- A discount brought the total below the minimum. For example, a $20.00 invoice with a 100% discount totals $0.00.
- The minimum was raised in settings and the invoice is below the new value.
- You are working in a different currency from your usual one. The minimum is entered in your account's default currency, so check the total against that figure.

Fix the total or lower the minimum, then save again.

## Should I raise the minimum?

Raising the minimum is useful if small invoices cost you more to collect than they are worth. Online card payments cost 2.9% + 30¢, so a $2.00 invoice would lose about 36 cents, or 18% of its value, in fees. Many businesses set a minimum of $10 or $25 for that reason.

You can also keep the default and rely on your judgment. The setting is only a guard; it does not change what you charge clients. If you need an invoice below the minimum for one occasion, lower the setting, save the invoice, and raise it again.

## Does the minimum apply to recurring invoices?

Yes. A recurring schedule creates invoices with the same rules as any other, so a generated invoice below the minimum will not be created. Review the schedule's line items under **Recurring** if runs are failing. Recurring invoices are available on Pro and Business plans. See Creating an invoice for building the invoice itself.
