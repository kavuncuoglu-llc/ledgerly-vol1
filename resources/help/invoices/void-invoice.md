# Voiding an invoice

Voiding cancels an invoice but keeps it on record. Use it when an invoice was sent in error or the sale will not go ahead, rather than deleting it.

## What does voiding an invoice do?

When you void an invoice:

- Its status changes to Void.
- The client can no longer pay it. The payment page shows a Void label.
- Automatic reminders and late fees stop.
- It no longer counts as money owed or as revenue in your reports.
- The invoice stays in your list for your records.
- The invoice number is not reused. If INV-4471 is voided, the next invoice is still INV-4472 or higher.

Voiding is how Ledgerly keeps your numbering clean and auditable. It is also useful for accounting, because a gap in invoice numbers can look suspicious, and a voided invoice explains it.

## How do I void an invoice?

1. Open **Invoices** and select the invoice.
2. Choose **⋯ → Void invoice**.
3. Confirm.

A Void mark is added to the PDF. The action appears in the invoice's Activity log with the date and time.

## Can I void an invoice that has been paid?

You can, but you should deal with the money first. If the client paid online, refund the payment (full or partial, within 90 days) so you are not left holding their money. If you recorded the payment manually, repay the client yourself. Then void the invoice. See Refunding a payment.

If the invoice is only partly paid, refund what was received before voiding, so your records agree with what happened.

## Can I undo a void?

No. A voided invoice cannot be turned back into a live one. If you voided the wrong invoice, create a new one or open the voided invoice and choose **⋯ → Duplicate**. The copy is a new Draft with a new number, containing the same client and line items.

## Should I void, edit, or delete?

| Situation | Best choice |
|-----------|-------------|
| Draft with mistakes | Edit it, or discard the draft |
| Sent invoice with a wrong amount | Void it and issue a corrected invoice |
| Client cancelled the order | Void it |
| Client will never pay | Void it, or keep it open while you pursue payment |
| Client wants a lower amount, still paying | Waive any late fee, or void and reissue |

Keeping a bad-debt invoice visible in your Aging report helps you remember who owes you. If you would rather not see it, voiding removes it from what you are owed. See Collections best practices for guidance, and Invoice statuses for how Void compares with the other states.
