# Edit, archive or delete a client

You can edit a client at any time. To stop seeing a client without losing history, archive them. A client can only be deleted if they have no invoices.

## How do I edit a client?

1. Open **Clients** and click the client.
2. Click **Edit**.
3. Change any field: name, company, emails, phone, address, currency, payment terms, tax ID or notes.
4. Click **Save client**.

Edits apply to new invoices. Invoices you have already sent keep the details they were sent with, so the client's records stay accurate. Draft invoices pick up the new details when you next open them.

## What does archiving do?

Archiving hides the client from the default Clients list and from the client picker on the invoice form, but keeps everything: invoices, payments, notes and statements. Reports still include their past invoices.

Use it for clients you no longer work with. To archive, open the client and click **⋯ → Archive client**.

## What are the effects of archiving?

- Existing unpaid invoices stay open, and clients can still pay them from their payment links.
- Reminders and late fees continue as usual on those invoices.
- Recurring schedules for an archived client fail to generate new invoices. End or pause those schedules first. See Pause or resume a recurring schedule.
- Archived clients still count toward the Free plan limit of 3 clients.

## How do I bring an archived client back?

1. On the Clients page, change the filter from **Active** to **Archived**.
2. Click the client, then **⋯ → Unarchive**.

The client returns to the main list with all history intact.

## When can I delete a client?

Deleting is only possible when the client has no invoices. This is mainly useful for a client added by mistake or created by an import. If a client has any invoices, even Void or Draft ones, the **Delete client** option is unavailable and you should archive them instead.

To delete: open the client, click **⋯ → Delete client**, and confirm. Deletion cannot be undone, but the client's contact details can simply be added again.

## Why can't I delete a client?

The Delete option is greyed out when invoices exist. Invoice history is part of your financial record, so Ledgerly keeps it. Voiding an invoice does not remove it. You have two options:

- Archive the client, which hides it from daily work.
- Export your data from **Settings → Account → Export my data** if you need a full copy.

**Can team members edit clients?**

On the Business plan, Owners, Admins and Members can edit clients. Only Owners and Admins can delete or archive them.
