# Multiple billing contacts for a client

Each client can have up to 3 billing contact email addresses. Invoices, reminders and receipts go to every address, so finance teams and managers can all see them.

## Why add more than one billing contact?

Many businesses want more than one person to see an invoice: the person who ordered the work, the accounts payable team, and perhaps a manager. Adding all of them to the client record means you only send once and nobody is forgotten.

## How do I add contacts?

1. Open **Clients** and click the client.
2. Click **Edit**.
3. In the **Email** section, enter the first address.
4. Click **Add another email** to add a second and third.
5. Click **Save client**.

The limit is 3 addresses per client. The **Add another email** link disappears once you have three. Remove an address by clicking the **×** next to it.

## What do the contacts receive?

| Message | Sent to |
|---|---|
| Invoice email with PDF and payment link | All billing contacts |
| Automatic and manual reminders | All billing contacts |
| Payment receipts | All billing contacts |
| Client portal login link | The address that requests it |

If you use Auto-send on a recurring schedule, the invoice also goes to all billing contacts. See Auto-send vs draft for recurring invoices.

## Which address is the primary one?

The first address in the list is the primary one. It appears in the "To" line of the invoice email, and the other addresses are copied. Drag the addresses to reorder them. The primary address is also the one shown in lists and reports.

## Can I send one invoice to a different person?

Yes. When sending an invoice, click **Edit recipients** in the send dialog and add or remove addresses for that email only. This does not change the client record. The change is applied to that invoice's email, and reminders on that invoice continue to use the client's saved contacts.

## What if an email bounces?

If one address rejects the message, the others still receive it. The invoice's **Activity log** shows "not delivered" for the address that failed, which usually means the client's mailbox rejected it. Correct the address on the client record and resend.

**Do all contacts get their own portal access?**

Each billing contact can request a magic-link login from the client portal, using their own email address, and sees the same invoices as the others. There are no separate passwords or permissions. See Client portal.

**Does this work with the Free plan?**

Yes. The 3-address limit applies to every plan.
