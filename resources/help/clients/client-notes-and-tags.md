# Client notes and tags

Notes let you keep private information on a client, and tags let you group clients so you can find them quickly. Neither is ever shown to the client.

## What are client notes?

The **Notes** field on a client is a free-text area for anything you want to remember: how they like to be contacted, the name of the person who approves invoices, agreed discounts, or context from a call. Only you and your team see it. It does not appear on invoices, statements, emails, the payment page or the client portal.

## How do I add or edit a note?

1. Open **Clients** and click the client.
2. Click **Edit**.
3. Type in the **Notes** field.
4. Click **Save client**.

Notes are plain text. You can write several paragraphs, and the notes are searchable from the search box at the top of the Clients list.

## Do notes show on invoices?

No. If you want a message printed on a client's invoices, use the invoice's **Notes to client** field or the invoice footer text. These are visible to the client, so keep private information out of them.

## What are tags?

Tags are short labels, such as "retainer", "agency", "slow payer" or "2026 project". A client can have up to 10 tags, and each tag can be up to 30 characters. Tags help you slice your list without creating separate folders.

## How do I add tags?

1. Open the client and click **Edit**.
2. In the **Tags** field, type a label and press Enter.
3. Repeat for each tag, then click **Save client**.

Existing tags are suggested as you type, which keeps spelling consistent. Remove a tag by clicking the **×** on it.

## How do I filter clients by tag?

On the **Clients** list, click **Filter → Tags** and select one or more tags. The list shows clients that have at least one selected tag. You can combine the tag filter with the Active and Archived views and with the currency filter.

**Can tags be used in reports?**

Tags help you filter the Clients list. Reports such as Revenue and Aging are organised by client and date, not by tag. To report on a group, filter the Clients list by the tag, then pick those clients in the report's client filter.

**Are notes and tags imported from CSV?**

Notes can be imported by mapping a column to the **Notes** field. Tags can be imported too: put several tags in one cell separated by semicolons. See Import clients from CSV.

**Who can see notes and tags?**

Everyone with access to your account. On the Business plan, that means Owners, Admins and Members.
