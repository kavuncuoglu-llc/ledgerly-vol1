# Client portal

The client portal gives each client a secure page where they can see all of their invoices and pay them. Clients do not need a password: they sign in with a magic link sent to their email.

## What is the client portal?

It is a private page for one client that lists all their invoices with Ledgerly: open, overdue and paid. From there they can open any invoice, download its PDF, and pay open ones. It saves clients from digging through old emails and gives you fewer "can you resend that invoice?" requests.

## What can a client do in the portal?

- See the status and amount of every invoice from you
- Open and download invoice PDFs
- Pay open invoices by card or bank transfer, subject to the methods you turned on in Settings → Payments
- See payment history and receipts for invoices already paid

Clients cannot edit invoices, see your notes, or see other clients. A client sees only their own invoices, never those of any other client.

## How does the client sign in?

There is no password. The client enters their billing email address, and Ledgerly emails a one-time magic link. Clicking it opens the portal. The address must be one of the billing contacts on the client record, or no email is sent. Magic links expire after a short time and can be used once, and the portal session ends after a period of inactivity.

## How do I give a client portal access?

1. Open **Clients** and click the client.
2. Click **Send portal link**.
3. Choose which billing contacts should receive it and click **Send**.

You can also copy the portal link from the same menu and send it yourself. Each invoice email also includes a **View all invoices** link at the bottom, which takes the client to the portal sign-in page.

## Is the portal available on every plan?

The portal view and signing in work on all plans. Paying from the portal requires online payments, which are on Pro and Business. See Accept online card payments. Without online payments, the portal is still a handy place to view and download invoices.

## Does the portal show late fees?

Yes. A late fee appears as its own line on the invoice and is included in the total due on the payment page, just as on the emailed invoice. Waived fees disappear from the totals.

**How do I stop a client using the portal?**

Archive the client, or remove their email address from the client record. A magic link is only sent to addresses currently on the record. See Edit, archive or delete a client. Paid and unpaid invoices of an archived client stay visible through existing payment links.

**Is the portal secure?**

Links are unique, traffic is encrypted in transit, and card numbers are never stored by Ledgerly.
