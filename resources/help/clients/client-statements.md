# Client statements

A client statement is a PDF summary of everything a client has been billed and has paid over a period, ending with the balance they owe. Statements are available on all plans.

## What is in a statement?

- Your business details and the client's name and address
- The statement period and the date it was created
- Each invoice in the period: number, date, due date and amount
- Each payment received, with its date and method
- Any late fees, shown as separate lines
- The closing balance due

It is a good way to settle a long-running account or to answer "what do I owe you?" in one document.

## How do I create a statement?

1. Open **Reports** in the sidebar and choose **Client statements**.
2. Pick the client.
3. Choose a date range: this month, last month, quarter, year or custom.
4. Click **Generate**.
5. Click **Download PDF**, or **Email statement** to send it.

You can also open a client and click **⋯ → Create statement** to jump straight to the same screen with the client already chosen.

## What date ranges are available?

| Range | Covers |
|---|---|
| This month | From the 1st of the current month to today |
| Last month | The previous calendar month |
| Quarter | The current calendar quarter |
| Year | The current calendar year |
| Custom | Any start and end date you choose |

Invoices that were issued before the start of the period but are still unpaid appear as an opening balance so the closing figure is correct.

## How do I email a statement?

Click **Email statement** and review the recipients. By default it goes to all of the client's billing contacts, up to 3. The email is sent from `invoices@mail.ledgerly.example` unless you use a custom sending domain on the Business plan. You can add a short message above the statement.

## Which invoices are included?

Draft invoices are left out, because they have not been sent. Sent, Overdue and Paid invoices are included. Void invoices are not counted in the balance.

**Are statements in the client's currency?**

Yes. A statement uses the client's currency, so all amounts match the invoices. A client has one currency at a time. See Per-client currency and payment terms.

## What is the difference between a statement and the Aging report?

A statement is for one client and is meant to be given to them. The **Aging** report is for you, showing all clients' unpaid invoices grouped as current, 1-30, 31-60, 61-90 and 90+ days overdue. Use the Aging report to decide whom to chase, and a statement to send the details to them.

**Can I export statements as CSV?**

PDF statements are on all plans. CSV export of report data is on Pro and Business.
